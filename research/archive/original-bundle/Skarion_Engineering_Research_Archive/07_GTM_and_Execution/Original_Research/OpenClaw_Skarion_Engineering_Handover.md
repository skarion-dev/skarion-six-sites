## 8. Bid and qualification filters (MUST APPLY BEFORE WRITING PROPOSALS)
Classify each as **PASS / FAIL / NEEDS CONFIRMATION**:
1. **Is it truly open?** Verify official/client page and deadline; include time zone, addenda, and submission method.
2. **Services fit?** Output formats, volume, turnaround, fieldwork, software, client standards, QA expectations, initial data quality.
3. **Can the company bid?** Correct legal entity; state or country registration; U.S. corporation requirements; business licenses; good standing; vendor account, SAM/UEI/CAGE, set-asides, certifications, bonds, insurance.
4. **Staffing restrictions?** Is subcontracting allowed? Are international/offshore workers allowed? Is a U.S. workforce or security clearance required? Can an agency apply, or must work be done by a named individual?
5. **Experience requirements?** Minimum years, public-sector references, specific product systems (WinGAP, ArcGIS Enterprise, Civil 3D, Esri certification). A preferred certification is not necessarily mandatory; check exact text.
6. **Licensed/regulated work?** Does it require a PE, PLS, architect, arborist, survey crew, local field visit or signed/sealed drawings? Do not silently assume qualifications.
7. **Costs and practical economics?** Insurance at submission, proposal fee, document printing and delivery, licenses, portal fees, platform commissions, staffing, travel, payment terms, performance risk, likely gross margin and available time.
8. **Conflicts and confidentiality?** No unauthorized use of current/prior employer materials, private client projects, or internal Teams discussions.

**Real negative example:** Bryan County, Georgia GIS parcel/WinGAP RFP (November 2026) expressly prohibits subcontracting, requires insurance at proposal submission, and needs specialized Georgia tax-assessment/WinGAP skills.
