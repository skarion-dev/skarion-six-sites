## Offer and buyer

Sell a review-ready batch of temporary construction and permanent utility easement sketch exhibits or parcel impact sheets. A land survey lead or right-of-way project manager supplies the approved parcel geometry, alignment and take limits, controlled drawings, parcel and deed references, approved descriptions where necessary, and client standards. Return native DWGs, review PDFs, a parcel index, and a log of discrepancies and revisions. Phase releases around the buyer’s survey or right-of-way approval. Typical repeat triggers are successive alignment packages, batches of recorded land interests across a corridor, and reissues when negotiations or revisions change the approved footprint. [MnDOT design-build right-of-way scope](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/designbuild/documents/online/DBv12.htm) · [Nisswa example scope and revision cycle](https://cms2.revize.com/revize/nisswamn/Document%20Center/Government/City%20Council/2026/Packets/2026%200317%20CC%20Regular%20Mtg%20Packet_Amended%20v2.pdf?t=202603171554220)

Start with survey or civil primes and land services firms holding utility, fiber, water and transportation corridor assignments. Treat jurisdiction, authorized workshare, personnel location and access to controlled records as offer-level qualifications. Keep the work at the drafting level. The buyer’s authorized professionals resolve boundaries, determine rights and the legal description, direct and approve survey work, and sign or seal professional products. Property appraisal, legal title opinions, negotiations, monuments and record filing are separate services. California’s rules are an example of a jurisdiction that explicitly addresses responsible charge and decisions about maps, plats and descriptions. [California Professional Land Surveyors’ Act](https://www.bpelsg.ca.gov/laws/pls_act.pdf) · [California responsible-charge rule §404.2](https://bpelsg.ca.gov/laws/boardrules.pdf)

## Decision and validation

Keep this as a specialized add-on for utility permitting or client-directed survey CAD. The evidence establishes repeated project demand, separable drawing hours, visible competing production firms and a documented team route. A standalone top-five slot would require direct evidence of a recurring queue of approved-geometry exhibits exceeding the buyer’s internal and authorized suppliers’ near-term capacity.

Offer a paid three-parcel sample from one approved alignment under one survey lead. Fix the drawing format and review rounds and have the buyer release the source geometry and permitted record set. Return individual sheets, a parcel index and exception log, with traceability to the buyer’s approved extent and naming standards. Track drafting time, reviewer time, rework, mislocated or omitted geometry, first-pass acceptance and price. Test repeat demand by asking the prime for release volume, expected changes, its existing supplier lead time, and permission to assign a larger batch.
