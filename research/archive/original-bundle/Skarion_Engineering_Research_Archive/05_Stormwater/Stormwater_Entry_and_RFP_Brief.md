# Stormwater | Subcontract entry strategy
**Basis:** earlier Traverse City RFP review and original uploaded solicitation (included). No independent addendum copy was recovered; addendum-specific interpretations are prior research notes and must be verified before relying on them.

## Traverse City, Michigan — Stormwater Asset Management and Water Quality Plan Update
Original RFP issued October 23, 2026; questions November 13; answers November 20; proposals due December 11, 2026 at 5 PM local via tcmanage@traversecitymi.gov. Proposed work March–November 2027. Original RFP asks for integrated 20-year plan, asset inventory and condition, H&H evaluation, water quality, CIP, maintenance, public outreach, funding, design standards, GIS and modeling files. Proposal: one PDF, max 25 pages, min 11pt; five relevant projects completed in past five years, references, staffing/location, work breakdown, schedule, T&M not-to-exceed rates and separate Task 11 fee. See original PDF for exact requirements and sample agreement.

**Recommendation: no-go as unqualified sole prime; pursue a properly licensed Michigan PE-led prime as a GIS/CAD production subcontractor.** Prior discussion noted possible acceptance of personnel/subconsultant experience in addendum, not a waiver of licensure. Verify official addendum, Michigan professional engineering authority, firm authorization, insurance, contract terms, references, data access and subcontracting/offshore permission. Do not bid a prime contract based on this note.

| Scope | Skarion production role | Prime/specialist role |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory/data | GIS schema, records, attribution, map production, QA | Verify assets, condition, criticality and performance |
| H&H | Terrain/data prep, model input formatting under direction | Select model, calibrate and interpret hydraulic results |
| Water quality/CIP | Exhibit drafting, GIS overlays, tables under direction | Recommendations, treatment selection, prioritization and costs |
| Public engagement | Graphics and map support | Stakeholder strategy, meetings and final report |
| Standards/permit | CAD exhibits and document production | Engineering design criteria, code/permit decisions, signoff |

Prior review referenced Esri Enterprise, Lucity/CentralSquare EAM and XP-SWMM 2016; validate exact systems from City documents/addenda. Build a sample inventory and train a specialist on EPA SWMM inputs under expert review.

## Other stormwater entry offers
Drainage and culvert inventory, BMP mapping, as-built conversion, model-ready GIS datasets, ESC plan drafting under technical direction, asset QA. Partner with licensed civil/stormwater firms; don't claim plan reviewer certification or PE authority without proof. Example Northern Virginia partner targets from prior reminder: NOVA StormPro (https://www.novastormpro.com/home), GeoEnv Engineers (https://www.geoenv.net/), Total Stormwater Solutions (https://www.totalstormwatersolutions.com/), Sorba Engineering (https://www.sorbaeng.com/services), GKY (https://gky.com/).
