## Review order

1. **CAD lead.** Check source control, drawing dimensions, layers, sheet numbering, title blocks, revision history, model and plot consistency, CAD references and exceptions.
2. **Data owner.** Confirm the source inputs and visible content are fictional or fully authorized, including metadata, coordinates and client details. Record rights for screenshots and source drawings.
3. **Business owner.** Confirm claims, public captions, domain and buyer acceptance wording. Approve public sample labels or client attribution in writing.
4. **Web and social editor.** Prepare accessible captions, mobile crops, file thumbnails and original post copy. Keep the credited references in this internal plan. Publish only approved Skarion work after review.
