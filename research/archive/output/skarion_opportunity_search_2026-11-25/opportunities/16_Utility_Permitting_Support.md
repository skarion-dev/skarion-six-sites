# 16 · Utility permitting support

**US procurement and prime teaming · November 25, 2026**

Start with **FiberTel’s Golden State Fiber program** for repeat corridor packages and **Traverse City’s Front Street force main** for a published design-team opportunity with named permitting deliverables. The first near-term qualification deadlines are **Brookings Harbor on December 2** and **Lexington and Placerville on December 7**. Placerville’s separate broadband contract gives the clearest documented precedent for subconsultants producing encroachment packages. No verified live prime quote specifically buys a stand-alone utility ROW permit drawing package that Skarion can price today.

**Highest priority · FiberTel.** GSCA confirms signing FiberTel for design and construction in seven project areas spanning about 1,065 miles. Program funding includes $110.9 million in bond financing and $185.4 million from the California Public Utilities Commission’s Last Mile Federal Funding Account. GSCA separately documents rolling encroachment filings, returned comments and design updates. FiberTel is the named design-builder. The authority’s publications do not identify which permits FiberTel still owns, who supplies desktop drawings, or whether there is an open and funded subcontract. [GSCA contract announcement](https://goldenstateconnect.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FOR-IMMEDIATE-RELEASE-GSCA-Signs-Agreement-with-FiberTel-to-Advance-Construction-on-Golden-State-Fiber-Network-in-Rural-California.pdf) · [GSCA board permit status, pp. 6–7](https://www.rcrcnet.org/wp-content/uploads/Meetings/BoardMeetings/2026/03252026/03252026_GSCA_Packet.pdf)

## Pursuit sequence

1. **Package the work.** Quote three to five routine road ROW packages from approved alignments, base mapping, standard details and the prime’s jurisdiction checklist. Deliver native drawings and review PDFs, a completeness matrix and a revision log. Price one reviewer-directed revision round. Escalate missing or contradictory inputs before drafting. The prime’s authorized staff handle field verification, survey and design decisions, seals, agency filing, and final approval. Request written approval of the subcontract and delivery location as applicable before using real project files. [Example VDOT fiber plan checklist](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/media/vdotvirginiagov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/land-use-and-development/vdot-fiber-utility-plan-checklist-2025_acc-2025-03-27.pdf)
2. **Work the date gates.** Use the Traverse City optional Nov 30 walk and Dec 10 questions to qualify a Michigan A&E bidder. Confirm Lexington’s attendance record before spending bid time there. For Brookings, check bidder or award status and scope consent through the Port. For FiberTel and EntryPoint, qualify live volume, the prime’s own staffing plan, reviewer, and the dates files can be released.
3. **Use Virginia BEAD for project discovery.** DHCD’s November 24 supplemental list is a pre-challenge input. The challenge runs December 1 to January 15. The Progress Hub and earlier awards list identify operators with assigned areas. Verify provider-specific construction and procurement before pitching permit batches. Historical FiberLync reporting names Byers Engineering and SourceOne and documents operator fielding for VDOT permit plans. Their current demand needs confirmation.
