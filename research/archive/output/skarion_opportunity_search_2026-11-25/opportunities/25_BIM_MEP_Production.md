## Pursuit sequence

1. Use the Miami conference and permitted channels to identify a qualified, non-conflicted integrator. Verify what the prime needs on its team, the approved data and operator locations, and the SBE lane. In parallel, qualify Massport only with a U.S. production plan, and use the DPR and Hunt Jacobsen contacts to find actual trade leads.
2. Ask WSP or NV5 which real work orders have field-verified inputs, released redlines and a funded queue. Separate available workload from full contract value. Obtain the current subcontract flowdowns, data and location terms, and the lead’s acceptance criteria.
3. Price one model, zone or discipline under the prime’s BIM execution plan. Define native Revit version, shared coordinates, families, model-element requirements, views/sheets, allowed edit list and any Navisworks clash tests. Return native model changes, sheet revisions, a delta and an issue register. Keep routing, code decisions, sealing, field verification and fabrication release with the responsible engineer or trade lead. Confirm accepted inputs, a named reviewer and approval before transmitting live model files.
