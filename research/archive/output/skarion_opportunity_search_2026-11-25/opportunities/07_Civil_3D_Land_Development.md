## Demand, supply and pursuit

Jasper, UConn and ESD establish demand for plans and site development at different scales. Jasper published **nine qualification packages** for predecessor RFQ 2023-02, one local measure of competition among engineering teams. UConn's self-performance requirement and ESD's certification and technical gates limit the parts an outside production firm could deliver. Coverage across the three bids establishes demand for broader engineering delivery. The number of available drafting subs and their capacity remain unmeasured. [Jasper archive](https://jasper-ga.us/notices.htm) · [UConn scope and qualifications](https://cpfp-procurement.media.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1051/2026/11/012.1-OC.CIVIL30-Solicitation-Information-Sheet.pdf) · [ESD solicitation](https://esd.ny.gov/sites/default/files/media/document/GO-SEMI-Site-Infrastructure-Consulting-Services-RFP.pdf)

1. **Jasper first.** Offer to revise three to five approved site, erosion-control or utility sheets. Deliver a clean checked DWG and dependent-reference package, plot PDFs and a marked-up revision register. Agree on the Civil 3D version, template, shared coordinates, Xrefs and approved workspace. The prime's licensed lead owns grades, drainage decisions, survey verification, codes, engineering QA, submission and seal.
2. **Use award timing.** If Jasper bid teams are closed, get the respondent list after December 1 and wait for the relevant work order trigger. Track UConn candidate firms at selection and target only work orders that permit outside production. Treat any quoted production value as an independent fixed scope priced after inspecting the package.
3. **Approach ESD teams for a compatible 30% package.** Match site exhibit production to the licensed team's direction and confirmed conflict status. Where specific credentials are relevant, ask the teammate to confirm certification and the intended participation classification. Ask AECOM separately about project-specific needs and its project-manager-led intake.
