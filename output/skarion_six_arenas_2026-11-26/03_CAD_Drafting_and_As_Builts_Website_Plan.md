# Skarion Drafting website and social plan

**Pursue now · Team review draft · November 26, 2026**

Lead with **record drawings from approved field redlines** for utility, civil and specialty contractors. Sell a bounded package of editable drawings, plotted sheets and a record of unresolved details. Engineering and survey teams with recurring drawing backlogs are a second buyer segment. Use **Skarion Drafting** as the working name and `/cad-drafting-as-builts/` as the parent-site route until the domain and brand are approved.

The competitive file covers **54 companies**, **18 documented project precedents** and **12 public business social examples**. It includes direct production specialists, field capture providers, engineering primes and one marketplace comparator. Buyer categories are planning judgments. The company links show published services and examples. The counts describe this benchmark set. See the [company comparison data](03_drafting.json).

## The service and the handoff

| Offer | Primary buyer | What comes in | What goes back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Record drawings and redline incorporation** | Utility contractor project manager or closeout lead | An approved redline set, editable base files or an agreed redraw source, drawing standards, sheet list and deadline | Updated native DWG files, plotted PDFs, drawing index and sheet-by-sheet exception log |
| **Legacy drawing conversion** | Facility record manager or engineering firm | Legible PDF, scan or paper digitization, scale references, layer template and expected level of detail | Editable CAD, plotted comparison and notes on unreadable or conflicting source geometry |
| **Civil and survey CAD production** | Civil designer or licensed survey practice | Approved field measurements or survey data, coordinate reference, units, template and drafting instructions | Civil 3D or AutoCAD production files and PDF sheets in the agreed scope, plus coordinate and missing-data checks for buyer review |
| **Ongoing CAD overflow** | Engineering CAD manager with a recurring queue | Approved first batch, agreed templates, shared issue queue, review cadence and defined utilization | Repeat production batches, revision logs and a weekly handoff to the buyer's reviewer |

Start with a **paid representative batch** of one to three sheets. Confirm sheet count, disciplines, complexity, base-file condition, revision allowance, deliverables and due date before quoting. Price an expanded batch or reserve capacity after the buyer accepts the first package. Assign field observation, design interpretation, survey determinations, engineering approval and sealing to the buyer's designated accountable professional. Skarion's drafter applies instructions and logs ambiguities for resolution.

Use **Scope a drawing batch** as the main button and **View a sample handoff** when the sample passes internal review. Immediately below the hero, show a sample handoff with DWG, PDF, index and issue log. Keep case previews and qualification links close to their relevant service. The landing page should introduce a repeatable workflow rather than publish a claim about delivery times or technical accuracy before it can be demonstrated.

On the first inquiry, ask for business email, buyer role, workstream, sheet count, source type, intended software and version, deadline and time zone, project location, and access restrictions. Ask whether field inputs are already approved and who will review the work. After initial qualification, agree the transfer channel, data location, recipient access, retention and any required NDA. Mark fields with a genuine privacy implication in the internal intake specification and send only the project brief until the file handoff is agreed.

## Manual launch checklist

1. **Owner and CAD lead** agree the client-facing offer, accepted software and version, template conventions, unit and coordinate checks, review roles, and minimum pricing inputs.
2. **Production** creates original samples and records time for input clarification, drafting, checking and corrections. Prepare one reusable deliverables manifest and one example exception log.
3. **Reviewer** checks model and plot alignment, sheet numbering, page geometry, text and layer standards, CAD references and open issues. Record which approved markup closes each change.
4. **Web and marketing** stage the service pages, attributed internal benchmarks, sample pages, request form, privacy handling and social drafts. Confirm all links, captions and buttons route to an owner.
5. **Business owner** reviews the draft and approves public claims, sample use and any domain purchase. Trial the offer on one qualified paid batch and use acceptance and rework data for the next scope decision.
