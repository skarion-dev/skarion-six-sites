## Offer and first test

**Skarion Survey CAD** sells repeat desktop production to survey and utility teams. Start with accepted survey-point imports, existing-conditions mapping, CAD from reviewed utility-locator or GPR markups, topographic drafting, redline revisions and drawing cleanup. The work uses Skarion’s existing AutoCAD, GIS, OSP utility mapping and production QA experience. Expand into complete boundary and ALTA production once an experienced survey CAD lead can supervise the workflow and the responsible licensed firm confirms the arrangement. The 50-hour practice pack can demonstrate a workflow and expose skill gaps. Treat each employer’s stated experience requirement as a separate qualification check.

**Paid pilot.** Ask for one redacted sheet or defined utility segment, one accepted point export or markup package and the firm’s CAD base, code/layer standard, units, coordinate system and responsible reviewer. Confirm compatible software and the permitted work and data locations. Agree the output and one review round. Return editable CAD, check PDF, a source crosswalk and open-item log. Compare an agreed set of source features and confirm software import and reviewer acceptance.

**30-firm test.** Build a campaign of 15 survey firms with topo or utility work, 10 combined civil/survey offices and five utility-focused survey or SUE teams. Start with the [buyer and prime queue](Survey_CAD_Prospect_Queue_2026-11-27.csv). Record the actual buyer type, backlog trigger, firm acceptance of outside capacity, required software, team location and permissible transfer. Use the fit evidence to draft a personalized message around a single paid sheet or segment. Measure qualified replies, technical objections, paid tests, first-pass acceptance, revision hours and margin after production, review, project management and software costs. Keep actions on demand.

## Professional and data boundaries

Use the descriptive working name **Skarion Survey CAD** and sell production support from accepted client data. The responsible party directs fieldwork and makes decisions about survey control, boundaries, GPR interpretation, utility identity, depth, confidence and final issuance. Preserve supplied quality labels and source provenance. New York and Florida supervision, entity authorization and sealing rules need an engagement-specific check before offering professional work. Defer complete ALTA production and certification to the qualified licensed team.
