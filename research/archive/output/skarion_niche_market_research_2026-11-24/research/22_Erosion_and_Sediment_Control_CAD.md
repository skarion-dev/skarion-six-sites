# Erosion and sediment control CAD

**Demand 3/5, medium confidence for engineer-directed production.** Buyers request recurring land-development drafting that includes erosion and sediment control (ESC), and municipal contracts pay for these sheets within broader design packages. **Supply tightness is 2/5 for the open drafting bundle, with medium confidence, and unknown for a qualified ESC-only desk subcontract.** Accessibility is medium through a civil firm with a named reviewer and explicit subcontract approval. The best use is a specialty within Civil 3D land-development production.

## Offer and buyer reach

Sell a bounded drawing package to a US civil engineering firm. Use the buyer’s current base drawing, georeferencing and CAD standards, approved best management practice (BMP) locations and details, and written engineer markups. Deliver native DWGs, PDFs, and a revision log for the engineer’s review. The client retains decisions about design, phasing, drainage calculations, permit submission, and sign-off. For a stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP), confirm the local permit, responsible operator and required qualified professional before taking drafting inputs. Field inspections and certification require separate capability and authorization. [EPA permit coverage and operator roles](https://www.epa.gov/npdes/getting-coverage-under-epas-construction-general-permit-waivers) · [California professional qualifications](https://water.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/stormwater/construction/general_permit_reissuance.html)

Start with New Jersey civil firms preparing small residential or commercial site sets. New Jersey’s program generally requires a plan for construction disturbance over 5,000 square feet, reviewed and certified by the jurisdictional soil conservation district. That creates recurring drawing and revision work as projects reach submission. Certification activity is a demand driver. The two New Jersey marketplace examples below show the closer signal that firms solicit outside drawing production. California can be a second geography through a firm with an assigned Qualified SWPPP Developer where required. Keep transportation assignments separate because DOT templates, workshare and plan stages add delivery requirements. Ohio DOT, for example, distinguishes sediment-basin material shown on its project site plan from additional temporary controls added to the contractor’s SWPPP. [New Jersey Department of Agriculture program](https://www.nj.gov/agriculture/divisions/anr/nrc/njerosion.html) · [California permit program](https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/constructionstormwater/) · [Ohio DOT January 2026 manual](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/transportation.ohio.gov/hydraulic/ld2/archive/2026-01-LD2.pdf)

## Campaign decision and paid test

Carry this as a Civil 3D land-development specialty. A firm with a confirmed revision queue in one soil conservation district could earn a pilot, particularly if its alternatives lack local template fluency or miss agreed dates. The evidence does not currently support a standalone top-five campaign. The worldwide bundle has visible competition, the narrow qualified pool has one early observation, and municipal demand generally purchases a broader professional package.

Offer one paid set of up to three client-directed sheets from one survey/base drawing, one approved BMP/detail set and one round of written engineer redlines. Return DWG and PDF with a revision log that flags missing or conflicting inputs for decision by the design owner. Measure elapsed time, production time, reviewer time, acceptance and rework. Advance if a buyer can name a recurring next batch and confirm the permitted workshare. The most valuable missing evidence is a recent buyer account of a quoted or paid separable ESC drafting order, the qualified alternatives it considered, and their response or turnaround times.
