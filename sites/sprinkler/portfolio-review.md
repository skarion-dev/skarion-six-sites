# Sprinkler CAD portfolio review

Review the redline case first. The three samples are schematic training exercises with fictional files. They demonstrate source handling, revision control and reviewer handoff.

| Example | What it demonstrates | Reviewer check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FP-101 approved redlines | Transfer one authorized markup and return a conflicting continuation for review. | Match M-01 geometry to FP-M1. Confirm F-02 remains open in the CAD, PDF and register. |
| FP-201 trade coordination | Preserve supplied fire-protection and HVAC layers and track a plan-view overlap. | Match the two input versions. Confirm the open elevation and disposition stay with the designer and trade lead. |
| FP-301 shop extraction | Reconcile two included IDs against an approved source register and keep a held item separate. | Match the R06 version, confirm SH-31C stays out of the extracted schedule, and keep release with the shop reviewer. |

The downloadable artifacts use editable DXF, vector PDF and CSV. To price native Revit, AutoSPRINK or another format, have the named reviewer check that tool’s actual file exchange, references, templates and output. The [market brief](../../research/archive/sprinkler/Sprinkler_CAD_Market_and_Launch_2026-11-27.md) and [training sprint](../../research/archive/sprinkler/Zihad_Sprinkler_CAD_50_Hour_Sprint_2026-11-27.md) explain the pilot scope and readiness gate.
