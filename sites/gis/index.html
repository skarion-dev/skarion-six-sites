Before you send data

What if the sources disagree?

We keep the conflicting references visible, record the question and send it to the designated reviewer. The agreed source hierarchy guides routine edits.

How do we exchange source files?

Start with a short scope description. After review, agree a transfer channel, access permissions and retention terms with Skarion.

Can you edit our production GIS?

A typical batch is an editable handoff for your review. Production changes, field verification, surveying and professional approvals require a separately agreed scope and responsible party.
