# Utility permitting support

US market evidence through November 24, 2026

Fiber operators and their design-build primes buy permit drawings, application support, and revision work as projects move from route design to construction. The strongest entry is to produce and track review-ready packages from a prime’s approved design and field records, with public examples of subconsultants doing this work and limited visibility into how many can meet a particular buyer’s requirements.

## Offer and buyer

Sell a fixed-scope permit production package to an OSP engineering manager, permitting lead, fiber design-build contractor, or regional network operator with a funded route. Start with routine local or state road right-of-way (ROW) packages. Produce a native CAD file and PDF exhibit from approved base mapping and route redlines, reconcile quantities and sheet references, index the required forms and attachments, and maintain a comment and revision log. Use the prime’s approved asset or permit tracker and return unresolved discrepancies to the prime. The prime reviews and releases the package. Use owner-provided jurisdiction templates, design dimensions, and accepted details for any road profile. Railroad, water, limited-access highways, sealed crossing calculations, and traffic-control design are separate scopes with their own approval path. [VDOT land use permits](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/land-use-and-development/land-use-permits/) · [Virginia fiber utility plan checklist](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/media/vdotvirginiagov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/land-use-and-development/vdot-fiber-utility-plan-checklist-2025_acc-2025-03-27.pdf)

Repeat demand comes from each succeeding route segment, locality, amended crossing, or agency comment round. A reachable buyer has approved routes or fielding inputs, identifiable weekly package volume, a prime reviewer, and permission to share records and delegate exhibit preparation. Start in a geography with a repeatable set of templates. A Virginia corridor could quote separately for routine VDOT/local ROW exhibit production and later evaluate another state. Maryland, for example, issues district utility permits under its own authorization and portal process. [Placerville phased work order](https://www.cityofplacerville.org/media/City%20Council%20Staff%20Reports/2025/04%2008%202025/12.3_AttB%20EPN_WO_01_v4_04032025_BP_FIN.pdf) · [Maryland SHA utility permit process](https://roads.maryland.gov/mdotsha/pages/Index.aspx?PageId=870)

## Implication for Skarion

Shortlist utility permitting for buyer validation. This niche has recurring named purchases, a documented subconsultant route, and a bounded initial deliverable. Exact subcontract scarcity remains unmeasured. The observed range for prime competition and providers advertising the same package make a general shortage claim premature. Keep the permitting offer distinct from front-end OSP routing, pole attachment and loading work, boundary or easement determination, and final as-built reconciliation.

**Pilot.** Quote one funded road ROW package containing up to three permit exhibit sheets in native DWG and PDF, the buyer’s completeness checklist, an exceptions list, and one reviewer-directed revision round. Require the approved route, base, field evidence, applicable standards, the reviewing engineer or authorized designer, and the permitted file and data handling arrangement. Let the prime handle any required site verification, design decisions, sign-off, and submission. Quote from internal production hours, reviewer time, expected revisions, and overhead. None of the reviewed prime award caps establishes a market price for this package.

**Decision evidence.** Ask approved prospects for a redacted set of recent permits, the number of similar packages in the next 90 days, queue time for first draft and revisions, how many current vendors can meet the standard, and the distribution of first-return comments. Track accepted packages, active production and senior review hours, rework hours, and the number of deficiencies caught before submission. Measure agency processing separately. A paid pilot plus an observed recurring queue and fewer available qualified production suppliers would support promotion to a top-five campaign.
