# One company, focused portfolios
## Structure
- Skarion Engineering: legal contracting entity (confirm exact legal registration, firm authority, insurance, W-9 and signatory), billing, PM, delivery, QA, client relationships.
- Four service identities: Skarion Drafting; SkarionGIS; Skarion Lighting; Skarion Stormwater. Keep one corporate LinkedIn presence initially. Use dedicated landing pages and collateral rather than separate incorporations. Consider Showcase Pages only when justified; platform rules should be verified before launch.
- Shared production pods: Utility GIS & Asset Intelligence; Infrastructure CAD & Permitting; Electrical & Energy Design Support. Stormwater starts as partner-led GIS/production support. Existing OSP is the anchor.

## Portfolio standard
Each specialty gets a modular, buyer-specific document: positioning; 5–8 deliverables; three technically reviewed samples; input-to-output workflow; software/QA; capacity and turnaround; paid-pilot engagement. Label each sample as actual authorized client work or demonstration. Never reuse confidential employer/client materials without consent or imply that an individual's prior work is a Skarion contract.

| Portfolio | Demonstration candidates |
|---|---|
| Drafting | PDF-to-DWG, redline set, as-built/Civil 3D base map |
| GIS | Asset schema migration, topology QA dashboard, utility conflict exhibit |
| Lighting | Parking lot photometric report, commercial interior layout, distributor fixture schedule |
| Stormwater | Drainage inventory, catchment delineation support, culvert database and model-ready dataset |

## Decision discipline
Earlier 30-day attention suggestion: Drafting 35%, GIS 30%, Lighting 20%, Stormwater 15%. Per niche exploratory targets: 100 qualified accounts, 60 personalized outreach attempts, five meaningful conversations, one demo, two qualified opportunities, one paid pilot. These are targets only; reduce to the team’s actual capacity. Review after 45 days; pause lanes without traction. No new permanent headcount simply because a market is large.
