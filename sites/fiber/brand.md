# Skarion Fiber brand kit

**Audience.** U.S. broadband operators and co-ops, engineering primes, and fiber construction teams. Lead with the OSP design manager or prime project manager who can define a route, file formats, and a reviewer.

**Positioning.** Skarion Fiber turns approved routes and field inputs into coordinated OSP drawing packages. Scope the first job around one route segment, specific deliverables, and one consolidated review cycle. Build a broader production relationship when the handoff works for the buyer.

**Name and domain.** Use **Skarion Fiber** in brand applications and **OSP fiber design** in headlines or search descriptions where the service requires explanation. `skarionfiber.com` is the proposed domain. Keep the existing
[Skarion Engineering OSP design page](https://skarionengineering.com/osp-design) in place while the team reviews the standalone site. Confirm the domain, ownership, redirect direction and canonical tags before publication.

## Visual system

| Use | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ink and hero | Fiber ink | `#182822` |
| Dark support panels | Forest | `#20382D` |
| Primary action and mark | Signal lime | `#DDF887` |
| Light surfaces | Paper | `#F5F7EE` |
| Supporting copy on light surfaces | Muted pine | `#4D6055` |
| Dividers on light surfaces | Plan rule | `#CBD5C9` |

Set dark text on lime and paper text on forest. Use lime mainly for actions, the mark and meaningful route lines. Use the standard system stack `Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif`. Use 700–750 weight with tight spacing for display type and 400 weight for body copy. A monospace system font can label actual drawing metadata or a schematic.

The original [route mark](logo.svg) joins three node endpoints with a route crossing. Pair it with the `Skarion Fiber` wordmark assembled in the site header. Leave space equal to one quarter of the badge width around the mark. Keep the badge at least 24 px square in full-color digital usage and use the simplified [favicon mark](logo.svg) with the original SVG at small sizes. For embroidery or vinyl, use the [one-color variant](assets/logo-mono.svg) in a sufficiently large proof first. Preserve proportions, the three nodes and the gap between paths. The social card is available as an [editable SVG](brand-assets/social-card.svg) and a [1200 × 630 PNG](brand-assets/social-card.png), alongside a [512 × 512 avatar](brand-assets/avatar.png).

## Images, voice, and claims

- Use corridor photos for atmosphere and original route drawings for technical explanations. A photo of a road and poles is a scene reference only. Keep client geography, coordinates, title blocks and personal information out of public samples until the rights and source records clear review.
- Write around inputs, deliverables and responsibilities. Good headline copy is “From approved route to review-ready plans.” Say “we log crossings that need review” or “return sheets and the material schedule under the agreed revision.” Describe licensed review and field authority as assigned in the agreement.
- The site uses the rural road photograph by Warren Valentine under the Unsplash License. The photograph depicts a scene independent of Skarion’s work. Local derivative `assets/rural-corridor.webp` is compressed and displayed with a responsive crop. [Photographer and image record](https://unsplash.com/photos/an-empty-road-with-power-lines-in-the-distance-zCwNtKYn8uQ) · [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)
- The route mark, mono mark, site icons, example process diagram and social card are original project assets. The diagram explains how route references carry through an agreed package. The public site makes no claim for named projects.

## Social and content production

Use the parent Skarion Engineering LinkedIn page for the first month. Package a 1200 × 630 single-image post or a 1080 × 1350 four-page carousel with the mark in a consistent corner. Use text captions for any screen recording and provide alt text for public graphics. Put a single call to action on each post. Publish an approved scope checklist before any case study.

| Week | Document post | Practical post | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five inputs for a route pilot | A missing field reference that stops a plan sheet | Request a pilot scope check |
| 2 | Original redline-to-revision sequence after approval | Which plan and quantity IDs must agree | Send a sanitized summary |
| 3 | HLD handoff into LLD | Reviewer-led 60-second walkthrough of a synthetic splice cross-check after approval | View the reviewed sample |
| 4 | File handoff and revision checklist | What one project issue log should capture | Request a scoped pilot |

Start with approved identity art and scope copy. The commercial lead signs off on pilot limits, the OSP lead signs off on technical claims, and marketing verifies image rights and contact routing. Track qualified inquiries, pilots quoted, pilots approved, first-review acceptance, and repeat orders when an approved measurement workflow exists. Site intake drafts email locally in the visitor’s email client.
