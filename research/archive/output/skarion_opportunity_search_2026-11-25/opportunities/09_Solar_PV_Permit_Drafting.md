# 09 Solar PV permit drafting

**United States · Verified November 25, 2026**

Start with **Willdan Energy Solutions on Manteca CIP 27015** for an awarded prime conversation and **Connecticut Green Bank’s cross-sector EPC RFP** for pre-award teaming. Willdan has a live project and has already procured a separate electrical installation package. Connecticut Green Bank expressly buys engineered permit sets and as-built PDFs within its EPC contract. Neither record establishes a current drafting-only subcontract. Rockville offers the clearest near-term rooftop and carport lead with a named preferred EPC and a scheduled award vote.

Offer a fixed scope for one site. Convert prime-approved site and array layouts, equipment schedules and electrical one-lines into native CAD and review PDFs, or update a record set from field-approved markups. Include a revision log and unresolved-items list. The EPC and its responsible engineers control design, field verification, calculations, sealing, utility coordination and permit submissions. Use the prime’s approved file environment and confirm subcontract approval and permitted delivery locations before any files are transferred.
