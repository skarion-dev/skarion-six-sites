# Skarion subcontract due diligence across 25 niches

**US opportunities · November 25, 2026 ET.** Each niche gets a fresh search for work Skarion can pursue through a prime contractor. The earlier market and buyer-procurement briefs supply candidate projects. Qualification in this pass centers on the actual buying firm, the desktop production package, the quote and decision process, and the conditions for delivering it.

## Check the evidence

- Recheck earlier open procurements and the strongest named award leads in the matching opportunity brief. Look for addenda, preproposal attendees, planholders, signed award notices, direct prime invitations, and any evidence of separate work packages. State what the record proves. A planholder or attendee is a possible bidder. An award establishes the firm and owner scope. Neither establishes an open subcontract.
- Search for new opportunities through firm planrooms and vendor pages, prime-authored trade or legal notices, SUBNet, agency subcontract outreach registries, and public project records. Distinguish an exact advertised package, a bundled or adjacent trade, a contingent quote collector, open specialty vendor intake, and a named project prospect. Use a dated historical lead when it has a real verification action. Record canceled, closed, and conflicting records without putting them in the live quote queue.
- For each actionable route capture the project and solicitation ID, prime, published decision or quote contact and role, work email and phone or official form, advertised scope, the Skarion pitch, the prime's cutoff and time zone, the separate owner cutoff, and certification, professional, site, data-access or place-of-performance conditions. A name from a separate business record can be a routing contact. Confirm project authority separately. Keep unknowns visible and do not estimate deadlines, contacts, subcontract fees, or certification status.
- Prefer the original posting from the owner or firm. Identify reproduced source documents and conflicts. Cross-reference a shared project to avoid treating it as multiple quote requests. No messages, quote submissions, account registrations, CRM edits, or other external changes.

## Deliverable

Write the assigned niche brief in this folder. Include four to eight useful opportunities where available, a disposition for earlier solicitations with meaningful relevance, and the first concrete pursuit steps. Report the strongest lead, the relevant deadline and zone, the exact public source, and whether the advertised work can be priced as a desktop-only package. Where there is no confirmed exact-scope quote, identify the closest verifiable route and what the firm would need to confirm.
