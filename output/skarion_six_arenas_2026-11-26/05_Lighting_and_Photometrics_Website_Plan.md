# Skarion Lighting · Lighting and photometrics website plan

**Internal planning handoff · November 26, 2026 · 30-day pilot**

Lead with a **paid, one-site photometric layout and fixture comparison** for electrical contractors, civil/MEP consultants and design-build teams. The buyer gets a checked calculation plot, editable fixture schedule and assumptions register from approved base geometry and fixture data. Start with a small commercial parking area where the project team supplies the applicable criteria and has a named reviewer. Set scope and fee before production. Use **Skarion Lighting** as the working name and `/services/lighting-photometrics` as the parent-site route, with `skarionlighting.com` as the preferred alias pending the portfolio lead's registrar and brand checks. Draft the landing page and proof cards internally before deciding public launch.

### Who to test the offer with

1. **Electrical contractors and design-build estimators.** Offer a comparable grid and fixture schedule for one quoted site. Ask whether their usual lighting rep already supplies a layout and when that route leaves a review gap.
2. **Civil and MEP project managers.** Offer overflow production from an issued base drawing, engineer-defined criteria and an approved fixture list. Prompt for the review stage, drawing owner and drop date.
3. **Lighting consultants and facility retrofit teams.** Offer a one-for-one or two-schedule comparison where they own selection and the supplier permits fixture-file use.
4. **Roadway and municipal primes.** Use these projects as scope intelligence for a later partner-led test. The prior Skarion diligence documented a City of Newport packet with VHB as proposed prime and an illumination scope for VN Engineers, and a West Allis council amendment for KL Engineering lighting circuit work. The buyer documents establish real workshares and design context. Reconfirm contract stage, review authority and access before treating either as a pursuit. ## Pilot package and acceptance

**Scope.** One small parking area. One issued site plan with a common coordinate system, known grading assumptions and bounded calculation areas. One reference layout plus one approved option on the same calculation settings. Include one consolidated reviewer-comment round in the pilot quote. A simple open high-bay floor can be the second sample or the second paid test once technical review verifies the modeling workflow.

| Stage | Required inputs or evidence | Expected output or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Site type, jurisdiction, deadline and time zone, reviewer and intended use, permissible data location, base plan revision, comparison purpose and availability of fixture data | Written scope and named reviewing party. Arrange private transfer before requesting drawing or fixture files. |
| Model setup | CAD or reliable scaled PDF, dimensions/scale check, property-line geometry, surface levels, mounting locations/heights and orientation, relevant obstructions and surfaces | Anchored base model and input register with file versions, measurement units and flagged geometry assumptions. |
| Calculation criteria | Reviewer's acceptance matrix, calculation planes and grid spacing by zone, units, metrics and criteria source, required property-line points, light loss and applicable existing conditions | Criteria record accepted by the reviewer before interpreting results. Capture initial versus maintained illuminance as required by the project. |
| Fixture data | Exact manufacturer, catalog code, optic, output, tilt, mounting method and original IES file for each option | Source-to-model and model-to-schedule match. Confirm substitutions and lighting equipment assumptions with the reviewer. |
| Handoff package | Two runs with common base and criteria, PDF overlay, plot legend and assumptions | PDF point-by-point grid and stats by requested zone, fixture schedule in XLSX, annotated CAD overlay when compatible, revision and exception register. Agree native model deliverable before quoting. |
| Reviewer acceptance | Confirm geometry, grid height, boundary sampling, fixture match, units, light-loss assumptions, labels and actual governing metrics | Record the units, reading direction and ratio definition for each grid. Flag areas requiring a decision, rerun agreed changes and issue an updated revision log. |

An IES file describes the photometric distribution used as a model input. Treat the output as predicted illuminance under stated conditions, with measured values coming from a separately scoped field task. Identify what is manufacturer-neutral in the engagement. Use that label where Skarion is paid for analysis, receives no fixture commissions and may evaluate the client's approved multi-brand choices. [IES explanation of photometric files](https://ies.org/education/learn-about-ies-files/)

The initial contract covers desktop production and decision support. Keep circuiting, controls design, field measurement, emergency egress, roadway design, regulated design and permit seals with the applicable qualified team unless separately scoped with a qualified reviewing professional. The client's responsible professional confirms applicable regulations and criteria, signs where required and decides whether the package can be used for permitting. A contractor or municipality's requirements may also determine site access and work location.

## Website structure and manual project queue

**Proposed headline.** “Site lighting layouts and photometric calculations for your review team.”

**Page build order.**

1. State the small commercial site pilot, named buyer and deliverables above the fold. Use the primary button **Join the lighting pilot review** and secondary **View a sample photometric package** after approval.
2. Show a PDF excerpt for the calculation grid, a fixture schedule preview and a revision crop with project title, units and two clearly attributed options. Show an actual versioned handoff structure.
3. Show accepted inputs, target fit, a simple intake-to-review timeline and what the receiving reviewer owns. Use a separate private upload path only after fit and data handling are clear.
4. Add FAQ entries for approved fixture alternatives, CAD and PDF inputs, native model files, how a fixture substitution is handled, criteria confirmation and reviewer sign-off. Present quote and delivery timing after the actual scope check.
5. Route through the shared parent form. Ask for the lane, company role, site type and location, target date and time zone, approved drawing and IES availability, intended stage, reviewer and a brief project summary. Record source campaign and the lane in the CRM. Define one owner to acknowledge the request and schedule a review.

Keep all three briefs in the internal queue. Record source and rights, authorship, reviewer, publication decision and artifact version for each piece. A sample that passes review should bear the label **Sample deliverables using synthetic inputs**. A client case needs written permission and approved credits or redaction. Maintain links to third-party cases in the internal benchmark queue so the team can study their evidence and design its own material.

Capture actual fee, modeling and QA hours, number of revisions, reviewer turnaround, reasons for wins/losses and qualified CTA actions. Where buyers consistently need the fixture rep's built-in service or require unfamiliar engineering approval, return to the team with buyer evidence and revise the scope. These are proposed 30-day working targets.

**Review ownership.** The lane lead owns fit and quote. Lighting reviewer signs the modeling and criteria checks. CAD lead owns editable drawing handoff. Web and content leads own staging, analytics and editorial QA. The portfolio lead records the manual publication decision and signs off on domain and brand selection.
