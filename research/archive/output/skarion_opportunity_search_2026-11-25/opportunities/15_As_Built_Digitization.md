## Pursuit sequence

1. **Qualify NV5 first.** Ask for the current legacy inventory, remaining sheet count by complexity, confirmed source files, approval authority and whether NV5 has a funded subcontract slot under 30396. Pair the request with one sample showing the source, editable layers, visual comparison and resolution log. Keep field validation and acceptance with the prime.
2. **Screen eligible deadlines.** Resolve KGM’s certification and desktop scope before Dec 2. For Massport, verify a named capture team, the U.S. staffing plan and applicable delivery system before Dec 3. Use A491 only for a bidder-confirmed drawing migration or QA package. The Fitchburg WI 2026-1210 lead is a scan, OCR and searchable-PDF purchase, so keep it out of the native CAD queue. [Fitchburg scope listing](https://www.procuretap.com/bid/387510)
3. **Offer a fixed-price records batch.** Quote three to five authorized sheets first. Establish scale references, CAD version, layers, XREF behavior, redraw inputs, how illegible or inconsistent information is flagged, reviewer response window and acceptance conditions. Deliver editable DWG, comparison PDF, file and revision index, and a short exception register. Apply the site-verification and security responsibilities of the underlying contract to the quote.

**Result.** The public evidence establishes multiple current purchases of records production inside wider programs and one adjacent prime-issued as-built invitation. An open prime request for a separable, eligible desktop digitization package remains unverified.
