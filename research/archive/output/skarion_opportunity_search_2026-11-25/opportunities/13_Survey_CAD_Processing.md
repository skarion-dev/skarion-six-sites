## Pursuit sequence

1. **Grand Junction first.** The buyer's open status and need for survey mapping and surfaces are verified. Category A funding, attendance-to-bid conversion and a separate production budget are unverified. Confirm an interested team's Category A or G submission and scope before the Dec 1 deadline. The previously circulated attendee names establish interest in the procurement only.
2. **LKC for the next actual batch.** The firm selection, proposed survey fee and scheduled CAD task are documented. Ask about owner execution, point release date, what remains unassigned and where drafting can run. In parallel, qualify KGM's current quote with the estimator only where the trade scope and certification are a fit.
3. **Give the survey lead a small paid unit to evaluate.** Take one redacted, approved point file and its field codes, units, datum, breaklines and drawing standards. Return native Civil 3D or Carlson DWG, a draft plot, preserved point IDs, a surface when specified and a source-linked issue log. Resolve discrepancies only through the survey lead. Have the prime maintain control, interpretation, reviewer accountability and seals. Confirm owner approval and data terms before any project files are routed externally.
4. **Use Santa Clara and Loudoun for repeat business.** Ask a licensed firm whether it can secure a named sub role on RFP 2026-14. Ask J2 and Rice about active task orders and the next field-to-finish backlog. Price only confirmed individual work packages. A public contract amount belongs to the full prime unless the buyer identifies a separate line.

Grand Junction, Massport and KGM also appear in related Skarion briefs. Each is one underlying procurement. No standalone live request expressly limited to remote point-to-native-CAD production was verified.
