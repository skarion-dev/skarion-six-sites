# Skarion Lighting brand kit

Skarion Lighting helps electrical, civil and design-build teams review site lighting layouts through a defined photometrics pilot. The buyer supplies approved geometry and fixture data. Skarion prepares a comparable calculation plot, editable fixture schedule and assumptions record for the buyer’s reviewer. The launch headline is “Plan the light before you build.”

## Logo and use

The logo combines an optical lens, a grid crosshair and a fixture point. Use [the amber mark](logo.svg) at 24 px or larger with at least one-quarter of the mark width around it. Use the [one-color mark](brand-assets/mark-monochrome.svg) for drawing title bars and light backgrounds. The [horizontal lockup](brand-assets/wordmark.svg) was drawn for charcoal backgrounds. Use the mark by itself for small avatars and say **Skarion Lighting** in ordinary text. Preserve the amber and charcoal pair, aspect ratio and clear space.

Use system sans-serif for UI and presentations, Georgia italic for a single emphasis phrase in display copy and Courier New for scope labels or diagram notes. The site uses local system fonts. In diagrams, pair color with line styles and marker shapes. In calculation exports, use the reviewer-approved units and include a legend and revision.

## Voice and social

Describe the work product, the input the buyer controls and what the reviewer decides. Use “predicted illuminance,” “documented criteria,” “approved fixture file” and “review package” where applicable. Keep code and permit claims tied to the responsible project professional and a confirmed project scope. Lead with a practical comparison question before introducing technical detail.

| Channel | Draft content | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent LinkedIn | Compare two approved fixture layouts on a common grid. A carousel can show plan version, fixture ID, assumptions and an annotated comparison after technical review. | Discuss a one-site pilot |
| Parent LinkedIn | Share a checklist titled “What to send before a site lighting comparison” with geometry, IES, mounting details and reviewing party. | Ask for the pilot intake checklist |
| Short video | Follow one substitution through the fixture schedule, grid and revision notes using an approved sample. | Discuss the comparison question |
| Proposal | Put the inputs, exclusions, outputs and one comment round on the first page. | Confirm the reviewing party and due date |

Use the [editable 1200 × 630 social card](brand-assets/social-card.svg) as the link-card source. Review the [posting PNG](brand-assets/social-card.png) and [512 px avatar PNG](brand-assets/avatar.png) before use. For square or portrait derivatives, keep the wordmark, headline and series label inside the center safe area. Use the parent LinkedIn account for the pilot posts and the shared inbox for intake.
