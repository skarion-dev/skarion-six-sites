# Skarion Stormwater brand kit

- **Working name** Skarion Stormwater
- **Domain candidate** `skarionstormwater.com`, with `skariondrainage.com` as a backup
- **Stage** 30-day service pilot, pending delivery and brand review

## Position

Stormwater inventory and field-record reconciliation for engineering primes, inspection contractors, and public works teams with a designated reviewer. Lead with the records the buyer already has and the handoff their team needs. The primary action is **Scope one basin**.

Use plain terms from the delivery workflow. Name the item, its source and the action the reviewer can take. Preferred terms include drainage GIS inventory, as-built sheets, inlet ID, crosswalk, exception log and prime review. Keep uncertain connections visible. Attribute field findings and professional approval to the party responsible for them.

## Mark and wordmark

The [branching basin mark](logo.svg) frames a three-node network inside a catchment outline. Pair it with two live text lines in the header, **Skarion** above **Stormwater**. Use the mark alone for square avatars and favicons. Preserve the plate and proportions. Leave at least one quarter of the mark's width free of adjacent content and keep the mark at or above 24 px in digital designs. Test avatar legibility at 32 px.
