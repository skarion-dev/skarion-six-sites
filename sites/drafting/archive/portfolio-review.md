# Skarion Drafting portfolio review

Seven synthetic drafting examples are staged for review. Each case pairs an original source with a finished drawing, editable CAD, matching PDFs and a register for unresolved items.

| Example | Draft handoff | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Utility trench closeout | UT-01 plan, UT-02 profile, UT-03 tie-in detail, source markup and change register | Confirm the 14-foot offset from 0+60 to 1+20, retain the source profile and route E-01 at 1+80 to the designated reviewer. |
| Legacy PDF to editable CAD | Raster source, LEG-01 layered CAD redraw, PDF and layer crosswalk | Check the 48-by-32-foot shell and keep the L-01 equipment tag open. |
| Commercial service court grading package | CV-01 plan, CV-02 profile, station CSV and E-CV01 review register | Reconcile both end elevations and the 0+60 change from 99.95 to 99.90 feet. Assign the east connection to the prime’s survey reviewer. |
| Tenant suite existing-conditions drawings | TI-01 floor plan, TI-02 opening detail and opening register | Verify the 48-by-34-foot shell and the four-foot D-02 opening. Route the head height to the field reviewer. |
| Mechanical room record drawing set | MR-01 record plan, MR-02 sleeve detail and field-markup register | Check the reroute and unchanged pump footprint. Hold the E-M01 sleeve height for field verification. |
| Two-sheet equipment pad addendum | AD-01 plan, AD-02 detail, bulletin AB-02 and change register | Confirm both sheets show 33 feet at R1 and A-02 stays open for supplier input. |
| Utility road crossing review exhibit | RX-01 plan, RX-02 schematic section and review register | Check the 60-foot station interval and use U-01 to route utility depth confirmation and clearance review. |

## Review before release

1. Open the editable DXF in the CAD application and version the client uses. Confirm paper-space layouts, fonts, layer visibility, plot settings, units and scale.
2. Reconcile the CAD and PDF sheet IDs, revisions and dimensions. Find each open item in its source and register, and confirm who owns the follow-up.
3. Check the final captions, contact routing and shareable preview with the site owner before publication.
