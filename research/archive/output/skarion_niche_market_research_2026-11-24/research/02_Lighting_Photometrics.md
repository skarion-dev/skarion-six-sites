# Lighting layouts and photometric reports

**Research cutoff November 24, 2026**

US buyers demonstrably purchase photometric reports, including repeat assignments built from buyer-supplied IES files. General layouts face both freelance competition and free supplier design services, so the most plausible paid offer for Skarion is overflow for distributors or engineering firms that need editable, mixed-brand work under their own review process. ## Reachable work

**Initial buyer.** The estimating or application-design lead at a US lighting distributor, retrofit contractor, small engineering practice, or specialist lighting firm. A plausible buying trigger is an upcoming submittal with a customer-specified mix of fixtures, a short-deadline substitution, or a backlog of comparable sites. Reach is strongest when the buyer regularly creates deliverables in-house or buys them separately, will share dimensions and product files, and can approve the work.

**Initial deliverable.** Start with one fixture-schedule revision and matching CAD/markup. Offer a point-by-point plot, summary, and editable model when the buyer supplies the drawing, mounting assumptions, current fixture IES files, and acceptance criteria, and a named reviewer is available. Use the buyer's units and revisions. Retail fixture sales, sports-light installation, field measurements, electrical-system design, code approval, and sealed engineering are separate scopes. ## Commercial conclusion

Give generic photometric layouts a low priority for the demand-versus-supply shortlist. The sharper candidate is one mixed-brand comparison and submittal revision package, sold as white-label overflow. Track demand separately for fixture schedule updates, neutral comparisons, and reviewed point-by-point models. A free single-brand layout can satisfy the first buyer's need in many routine jobs, so confirm the actual reason to buy before scoping a trial. [RAB services](https://info.rablighting.com/lighting-design) · [Independent offer comparison](https://photometricbureau.com/services)

**Decision evidence with highest value.** In discovery with US distributors or retrofit contractors, record purchased layouts or revisions from the prior 90 days, their typical supplier count, why available product support failed, expected turnaround, price actually paid, and how often the same need repeats. One paid pilot should capture production, reviewer, and rework hours. Use those figures to measure contribution per senior reviewer hour. At a $50 posted budget, even three total delivery hours yield only $16.67 gross per hour before fees and costs. That example is sensitivity arithmetic, not measured production time. [$50 marketplace posting](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Photometric-Plan-for-Lighting-Design_~022124943550307130014/)

For a first paid test, supply a schedule and annotated layout for one defined site, with one review cycle and an issue log. A second option compares two buyer-selected fixture packages in one unchanged model and delivers the updated schedule, editable source file, and reviewer-approved comparison. Verify software access, drawing permissions, and licensed review where the project calls for it before quoting a photometric or approval-related scope.
