## Handoff and approval

1. Offer lead assigns demonstrator author, technical reviewer and marketing approver. Record the author, version, source rights and approval date for each asset.
2. Technical reviewer independently checks station references, units, addenda, alternates, owner quantities and scope exclusions. Keep measured quantities and allowances separate.
3. Marketing confirms that the project is labeled synthetic or documented with written display permission and actual Skarion scope. Remove all identifying plan data and confidential quotes before approval.
4. Site owner publishes approved marked plan and worksheet excerpts alongside the related service. Keep the full workbook private if ownership, proprietary methods or client confidentiality require it.
