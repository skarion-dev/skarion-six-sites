# Survey CAD processing

**Demand 4/5, medium confidence. Supply tightness 2/5, medium-high confidence for standard marketplace production, with qualified capacity for private client-specific overflow unknown.** US survey firms advertise repeat outsourced point-to-drawing work, and one close listing shows an actual hire. Standard survey drafting draws many proposals and has established white-label suppliers. **Skarion accessibility is medium**, conditional on a compatible CAD workflow, buyer acceptance of a firm subcontract, and jurisdiction-appropriate direction from the surveyor in responsible charge.

## Offer and route to the buyer

Sell a source-controlled **point-file to native Civil 3D or Carlson draft** to licensed land surveying and civil/survey firms. Start with existing-conditions production from buyer-approved points, units, datum, field-code library, breaklines and drawing template. Return preserved point IDs and elevations, layered linework, a buyer-directed surface when in scope, a draft PDF, and an exception log. The buyer resolves contradictory field notes, missing shots, control changes and boundaries. Keep title and easement opinions, construction staking, field measurements and certifications outside this first package. A repeat trigger is the next batch arriving from an active field crew. Civil 3D already connects coded features and assigns layers and styles, so the value is clean handling of exceptions, an editable handoff and fewer senior-review corrections. [Autodesk field-code workflow](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Civil3D-UserGuide/files/GUID-2DC2AA57-057B-41AC-BA2E-C893FF01A300.htm)

Reachable buyers are survey operations managers or firm owners who already direct licensed work and accept a disclosed external drafting team. Favor a private overflow engagement before field-heavy public prime contracts. Most marketplace examples are contract-to-hire or individual-technician listings. They support willingness to buy a defined scope but do not establish that the same buyers accept an agency, overseas production, or a B2B rate.

## Delivery gates and campaign decision

A Virginia launch needs an appropriately authorized survey practice where the work falls within land surveying. Virginia's professional responsibility rule requires direct control and personal supervision for nonexempt work, and New York's statute provides for qualified assistants under a licensee who retains responsibility. Florida's board rules address survey content, datum, control and responsible charge. Verify that the intended subcontract and supervision arrangement is permitted before accepting each jurisdiction. A final review alone should not be used to justify independently resolving survey matters. Treat the February 23, 2026 ALTA/NSPS standards as a separate qualified scope and ask for applicable Table A selections if a buyer later requests this work. [Virginia professional responsibility](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title18/agency10/chapter20/section740/) · [New York Education Law § 7208](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/EDN/7208) · [Florida reporting standards](https://www.fdacs.gov/content/download/21299/file/5J-17.051.pdf) · [2026 ALTA/NSPS standards](https://www.alta.org/policies-and-standards/policy-forms/download?formID=721&type=pdf)

**Keep as a demand-backed drafting specialization.** The evidence does not support an independent top-five position on low supply. Route survey clients separately from generic drafting if they confirm frequent batches and delays with their current providers. The highest-value check is whether a local licensed principal has recurring paid overflow that current staff and vendors cannot deliver on time, and what QC effort the buyer must spend when a vendor takes a batch.

Offer a paid fixed-scope test of one small site or an agreed subset of a completed job. Obtain a redacted reference drawing, approved point data, control, version, templates, layers, and rules for building a surface. Deliver native DWG, a draft plot, and a discrepancy list that cites source points, with one defined correction round. Measure calendar time, minutes of senior review, corrections, and contribution per reviewer hour. Require matching approved units and datum, preserved source coordinates and IDs, usable native objects, and traceability for open issues. Quote a recurring batch only after the surveyor's comparison to its completed drawing and a verified production-time baseline.
