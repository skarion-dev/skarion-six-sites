# Outside plant fiber design production

**Demand 4/5, medium-high confidence. Public design-prime supply tightness 3/5, medium confidence. Supply tightness for the remote production subcontract is unknown.** Public network owners are buying route design and construction drawings in multiple markets, with some issuing multiple phased assignments. Skarion has a **medium-access** opportunity through a qualified engineering or deployment prime with a confirmed design backlog, an approved subcontract, and authority to provide source data and project standards.

## Offer and repeat trigger

Sell one fixed-fee work package converting a buyer-approved high-level design and field-verified route inputs into review-ready detailed aerial or underground construction drawings, route and asset schedules, bill of materials, splice sheets, and an exceptions log. Deliver editable CAD and the agreed GIS schema where requested. Keep the buyer's design authority responsible for technical decisions, field validation, coordination, acceptance, and signed or sealed deliverables where the contract requires them. Standalone permit packages, pole loading and make-ready, and post-construction closeout are separate research niches.

Lead buyers are fiber engineering primes with several assignments, regional internet service providers with multiple funded build areas, electric cooperatives delivering broadband, and contractors holding design-build awards. Common repeat triggers are a new serving area, a parallel construction phase, an extension, and an approved pre-construction design revision. Reach means the prime delegates the production work and authorizes the delivery location, tooling, and access to project files. The named award alone establishes neither a current opening nor available subcontract spend.

## Campaign decision

Advance for **top-five validation**. Demand is stronger and the deliverable more separable than grant totals or generic drafting listings imply. The available proposal counts support a segmented prime market with some small fields. The decision depends on finding a prime with repeat funded drawing packages and a bottleneck among its approved suppliers.

Offer a paid pilot for one serving-area or route segment, capped at five drawing sheets. Work from a supplied design basis, surveyed or field-verified inputs, and a reference drawing set. Return review-ready native CAD/PDF, the agreed quantity and splice schedules, and a reconciliation log. Evaluate accepted first-pass sheets, buyer engineering review time, quantity-to-drawing agreement, input exceptions, and turnaround. Before taking production, confirm the owner's data handling rules, approved inputs and tools, permitted delivery location, and authorized signer or reviewer. The highest-value discovery evidence is a recent task order where the prime delegated this exact scope, along with how many approved vendors could accept a package on the current schedule.
