## 30-day training
- Days 1–5: lumens, lux/foot-candles, uniformity, glare, IES/LDT files, drawings; DIALux fundamentals.
- Days 6–12: DIALux evo CAD import, fixtures, outdoor surfaces, reporting; parking lot and pedestrian path.
- Days 13–19: AGi32 CAD import, grids, luminaire placement, report production.
- Days 20–25: AutoCAD layers, legends, schedules, redline revision and QC; conduit/circuit documentation only under direction.
- Days 26–30: three original reviewed samples, rate card, exclusions, QC and freelancer test.
Training resources: https://community.dialux.com/essentials ; https://www.dialux.com/en-GB/academy ; https://lightinganalysts.com/training-classes/ ; https://www.autodesk.com/learn ; https://elearning.ies.org/products/lighting-fundamentals . Training does not confer professional engineering authority.

## Buyer segments / outreach
Priority: distributors/manufacturers, small MEP firms, LED retrofit contractors, electrical contractors, landscape architects, architects. Explore US first; UK and EU distributors/design firms are possible markets, but validate local standards, marketing rules, software and approval requirements separately. Prior email suggested 40% marketplace / 60% direct B2B; weekly targets 10–15 qualified proposals, 40 researched companies, 30–40 personal emails, 20–30 follow-ups, 2–4 discovery calls. Targets, not predictions.

Draft: Hi [Name], I noticed [Company]'s commercial lighting work. We're building a technical production team for CAD lighting layouts, photometric reports and drawing support. Would additional production capacity be useful when your team has overflow? Happy to share a sample.

## Costs and gates
Planning estimates: engineer $600–1,200/mo; training/software $100–500; sales tools $100–250; reviewer reserve $200–500; total fixed $1,000–2,450/mo excluding manager time, legal/compliance, unusual licenses and job-specific freelancers. Original 90-day planning ceiling $5,000 requires actual budget approval. Example: 6 × $450 revenue = $2,700; less $720 freelancers and $1,400 fixed = $580 before unmodeled overhead/taxes.
Day 30: hire, three reviewed samples, price/QC, three vetted freelancers. Day 60: 100 researched prospects, 20 qualified proposals, aim for first paid pilot. Day 90: target three paid assignments and repeat-work discussion. Stop/reposition if no credible traction. Avoid stamped engineering, code certification, complex distribution or life-safety scopes until legally qualified.
