# Sprinkler CAD market and launch brief

Research checked November 27, 2026 ET. Working name **Skarion Sprinkler CAD**. Public descriptor **Fire sprinkler drafting and BIM support**.

## Where to enter

Start with licensed sprinkler contractors and authorized design teams that already have an accepted drawing or model, written markups, a named sprinkler designer and an overflow backlog. Offer one paid redline or record-drawing sheet. The contractor sends the base revision and drafting instructions. Skarion returns the agreed editable file, check PDF and an issue register. The responsible project team decides any unresolved changes and issues the work.

| Priority | Scope to sell | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First paid test | Redline incorporation and record drawing updates from an existing approved design | Match revision IDs, layers, references and tags. Route missing or conflicting instructions to the named designer. |
| Next | BIM coordination support and approved changes | Agree native versions, linked models, coordinate setup, naming, issue method and the review owner. Use the client's disposition for any reroute or model revision. |
| After a shop workflow trial | Shop drawing and schedule extracts from an approved model | Have the shop lead verify dimensions, item counts, joint assumptions, hold status and final release. Show a proof on one zone before promising repeat production. |
| Future, under a qualified specialist and authorized entity | Complete system layouts, equipment selection, hydraulic calculations and permit work | Confirm the specialist’s scope-specific experience, contract and jurisdictional authority before advertising or accepting the work. |

**Target buyer.** Start with the design manager or operations lead at a sprinkler subcontractor. Approach the VDC manager where a live trade-coordination backlog is visible. Qualify source access, software and staffing location, designated reviewer, expected turnaround, and whether the firm actually uses outside drafting. A contractor’s hiring post and a consultant’s case study are demand signals of different strength.

## What 50 hours can establish

Use the [50-hour sprint](Zihad_Sprinkler_CAD_50_Hour_Sprint_2026-11-27.md) to test whether a drafter can reproduce approved source changes, check a set, and route unknowns without creating extra design decisions. NFSA’s pathway lists about 25 hours of self-paced Fundamentals and a separate three-day instructor-led Application component. Plan the full pathway around its published schedule. NICET lists minimum relevant experience of six months for Level I, two years for Level II and five years for Level III, with defined core experience and other requirements. [NFSA pathway](https://member.nfsa.org/Events/layout-technician) · [NICET requirements](https://www.nicet.org/certification-programs/electrical-and-mechanical-systems/water-based-systems-layout/certification-requirements/)

Choose the software used by the first plausible customer. Start with manual redline work in their AutoCAD or Revit workflow. If they use AutoSPRINK or HydraCAD, verify the required version, authorized seats, file references, parts data and review methods before promising a native handoff. Revit/Navisworks issue coordination and conversion of a sprinkler model do not by themselves prove native sprinkler design or calculation competence. [AutoSPRINK library](https://autosprink.com/training/library) · [Autodesk coordination workflow](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2025/ENU/Revit-Collaborate/files/GUID-84E77680-25EB-4540-AB45-1C63416D3AE5.htm)

## Scope and name

**Skarion Sprinkler CAD** describes the production work and leaves room for both 2D and BIM. **Skarion Sprinkler BIM** makes sense if the first accepted contracts are consistently model-based. **Skarion Fire Protection Drafting** is broader and may suggest other trades that are outside this pilot. This is naming work. Review market and trademark clearance before adopting a public domain.

Project teams select the applicable standard and edition based on the job and authority. Introduce NFPA 13, 13R and 13D as distinct references during training. As an example of jurisdictional variation, Florida distinguishes design concepts and contractor layout. Texas identifies the responsible managing employee licensing classes for planning and related activity. Have the customer confirm the applicable entity, individual authority, project supervision, and delegated scope before offering regulated work. [NFPA standards overview](https://www.nfpa.org/news-blogs-and-articles/blogs/2024/03/12/comparing-nfpa-13-nfpa-13r-and-nfpa-13d) · [Florida Chapter 633](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/Chapter633/All) · [Texas State Fire Marshal](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/fire/information-fire-sprinkler-registration-license-test.html)

## Pilot economics and proof

Quote an agreed small batch, one review round, named reviewer and handoff versions. Calculate actual production hours, senior review, coordination time, correction time and software allocation. Compare contribution margin with the next batch quote before committing units or turnaround. Track first-pass acceptance, unapproved geometry changes, open-item accuracy and revisions per sheet. Keep the first demonstration and fold-out case as a template for asking contractors about upcoming authorized overflow.
