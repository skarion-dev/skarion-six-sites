# Drafting market-entry brief
**Source:** original November 10 market-entry report and the two datasets recovered from the emailed attachments. Start with the full report in Original_Research; the CSVs are original data, not freshly qualified leads.

The report identified 100 Upwork job IDs (30 posts showed hires; 56 priority-one fit), 100 distinct B2B companies (27 A-fit). It did not identify all winning bidders, accepted prices or private selection reasons. B2B names are prospects, not proven buyers. Demand categories in the report include architectural, floor plans, millwork, as-built, conversion, site, interior, redline, shop drawings and Revit work.

## Offer
Convert marked-up PDFs, measured sketches, redlines and record drawings into editable CAD using the client’s layers, scales, title blocks and QC standards. Prioritize architects, interiors/remodelers and civil/infrastructure firms with experienced design leadership. Offer a small paid trial and recurring overflow capacity. Add higher-risk design or fabrication detailing only with qualified review.

## Execution
Zihad owns delivery of a dedicated portfolio, scope/rate card, sample QA checklist and buyer shortlist. Read the report's eight-week training and 90-day launch plan. Start by qualifying five named prospects; identify CAD/production managers; send two individually reviewed introductions. Recent reminder leads: IMEG/christopher consultants, Gordon, Pennoni, Timmons Group and Bohler (outsourcing interest unconfirmed).
