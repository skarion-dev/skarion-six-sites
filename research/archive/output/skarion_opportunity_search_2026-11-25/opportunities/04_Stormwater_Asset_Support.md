# 04 — Stormwater asset mapping and engineering production support

Checked November 25, 2026. **Two open buyer solicitations, two awarded prime leads, and one award watch.** Traverse City is the clearest bid-time fit. Essex County is the clearest identified post-award target. The public records establish what the owners are buying and who holds the named awards. A payable subcontract still needs a willing prime, a budget, approved data access, and written scope. No direct quote request for a standalone desktop package surfaced in this search.

## Open buyer solicitations

### Traverse City, Michigan — Stormwater Asset Management and Water Quality Plan Update

**Open buyer solicitation. Due December 11, 2026 at 5 p.m. ET.** The city’s bid page lists the original RFP and one addendum. The November 13 question deadline has passed. The original requests a consolidated stormwater plan with current infrastructure information, condition and criticality assessment, system evaluation, and capital planning. The addendum requires signed acknowledgment with the bid. It allows attributed previous-employer references and clearly attributed subconsultant experience for portions of the qualification requirement. The proposal sheet requires authority to practice professional engineering in Michigan. The sample agreement requires prior written city consent for subcontracting and a written subcontract with applicable terms flowed down. The owner posts no dollar ceiling for the current RFP in the reviewed documents. [City bid register](https://www.traversecitymi.gov/government/city-departments/city-managers-office/city-bids-and-rfps.html), [original RFP and sample agreement](https://www.traversecitymi.gov/userfiles/filemanager/a0t9uifi5r8izmt89jr4), [Addendum 1](https://www.traversecitymi.gov/userfiles/filemanager/0q1v4jtypgujvc8s3bks)

**Prime route.** OHM Advisors has documented experience on the city’s earlier stormwater plan and worked on the prior GIS inventory. Hubbell, Roth & Clark has a Traverse City office and documented Michigan drainage GIS experience on Red Run. These are qualified firms to check for participation. Their bids and any open workshare remain unverified. The buyer has an Esri enterprise environment and Lucity. Asset data requests go through city staff. The selected consultant will receive the owner’s GIS and available video data. The legacy model is XP-SWMM 2016 and additional investigation may be needed on major trunklines. [Prior city plan appendix](https://www.traversecitymi.gov/userfiles/filemanager/hitshbwqubgkdrojdjcx/), [Red Run board packet documenting HRC’s GIS work and offices](https://cdn.oaklandcountymi.gov/oakland-sitefinity-prod/docs/default-source/water-resources-commissioner/water-resources-commissioner/resources/public-meetings/intercounty-drainage-boards/2026/june-17-2026-red-run-intercounty-drain.pdf), [owner questions and answers](https://www.traversecitymi.gov/userfiles/filemanager/0q1v4jtypgujvc8s3bks)

**Paid package to propose.** Reconcile one prime-supplied subcatchment or trunkline sample against approved as-builts and existing GIS. Deliver a staging layer, source and asset-ID crosswalk, topology and missing-attribute exceptions, and a queue of records for local verification. Keep owner GIS edits, field investigations, model judgment and condition sign-off with the prime and owner. Before pricing a live batch, confirm the prime’s bid intent, a proposal slot and the city’s consent and reviewer requirements.

**Open buyer solicitation. Questions due December 3 and qualifications due December 15, 2026 at 2 p.m. ET.** Qualifications require a New Hampshire PE, three recent project references, a description of subconsultants where applicable, and both a hard copy and a PDF on a thumb drive. Ask for the prime’s team requirements before the RFQ deadline and quote against a funded assignment and owner-approved workflow. Federally funded task orders may carry additional requirements. ## Pursuit sequence

1. **First deadline.** Start with OHM and HRC as Traverse City candidates. Confirm bid intent and whether a scoped GIS production line can be named. Prepare an asset-ID crosswalk and exception log sample using synthetic data for a bid team to review. Price from an approved source sample and a defined asset count.
2. **First confirmed prime.** Qualify Boswell’s active delivery schedule and capacity requirement. Ask for one paid, reviewable batch and the county approval and data terms that apply.
3. **Second deadline and the award watch.** Work Manchester in parallel through a pavement-qualified NH prime. Verify Charlotte’s minutes or agreement before treating Moore & Bruggink as the contract holder. Recheck the public register for new addenda immediately before any bid-related action.
4. **Secondary project.** Pursue HRC’s Red Run parcel/ROW production only after the team identifies a current GIS deliverable with budget. Keep Manchester’s non-bridge culverts as a separately tagged opportunity and count its RFQ once. Floodplain and FEMA map work require their own sourced demand and are outside these stormwater asset-package counts.

The Charlotte bid list is one observed supply measure and shows enough prime competition to question a low-supply claim for full-scope municipal inventories. The count of qualified suppliers for a bounded desktop subcontract and their available capacity remains unknown. Track how many primes acknowledge a schedule bottleneck, ask for a priced sample, and buy a batch before calling that segment undersupplied.
