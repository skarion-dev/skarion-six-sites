# Skarion Estimating brand kit

**Owner for approval:** Estimating offer lead and Skarion Engineering marketing lead. **Launch phase:** First-wave sales. **Proposed domain:** `skarionestimating.com`. The domain and registration belong in the shared launch checklist.

## Positioning

Skarion Estimating is a bid-desk extension for fiber contractors, wet utility contractors and civil construction primes. Lead with plan-traceable quantities, marked drawings and a clear record of bid assumptions. Start with one released work package and confirm schedule after scoping.

- **Service line:** Fiber and utility takeoffs.
- **Headline:** Bid-ready takeoffs for fiber and utility contractors.
- **One-sentence intro:** Send your current plan set and bid schedule for an editable, sheet-traceable takeoff your estimator can review.
- **CTA:** Request a scoped takeoff.
- **Avoid:** Win-rate claims, fixed turnaround claims and language suggesting that Skarion sets or submits the final bid. Refer priced work to an experienced estimator with access to approved local rates and work methods.

## Logo and layout

The [square mark](logo.svg) uses measured bid rows and two station ticks. It works as an icon and favicon. Use the [horizontal wordmark](brand-assets/wordmark.svg) for proposals and slide covers. On the site, the wordmark pairs the mark with live text. The amber tile stays visible on dark and light surfaces. Leave clear space equal to one quarter of the mark's width. Minimum web mark size is 28 px. Keep the tile upright and preserve its proportions. Pair “Skarion Estimating” in title case with either the company family name below or a plain parent-company signature in the footer.

| Role | Name | Value |

| CTA and selected measurements | Bid amber | `#F4BC43` |
| Main light background | Drawing paper | `#FAF8F2` |

Use Segoe UI with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks for headlines and copy. Use Consolas or the platform monospace for plan revision IDs and field labels. Keep headings short, use amber as a functional highlight and rely on navy text on amber. Align charts and tables to the same unit columns used in takeoff work. Color can support status, paired with explicit text.

Use a technical reviewer for every visible quantity and convention. Keep project names, title blocks, plan geometry and supplier rates private unless the owner has cleared them. ## Voice and channel copy

Use the words contractors use: bid item, station, addendum, work limits, allowance and exclusion. Write in short active sentences and illustrate the exact output. Specify who makes choices about production rates, risk and final pricing. Speak to the bid lead and estimating team directly.

**LinkedIn description:** Plan-traceable takeoffs and bid desk support for fiber and utility contractors. We organize quantities by sheet, station and bid item, and hand off editable schedules and marked plans for your estimator to review.

**Email opening:** If you have one fiber or utility package due soon, we can review the plan revision, bid schedule and due date and confirm availability for a scoped takeoff.

| Week | Proof post | Practical post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “Where did the 640 LF come from?” Pair the fictional conduit row with the marked route and station pair. | Share the released revision, bid form, work limits, due date and time zone needed to scope a takeoff. |
| 2 | Show a reviewed synthetic profile excerpt with pipe size, two depth bands and an allowance labelled separately. | Explain which bid schedule rows can be measured and which need owner or contractor input. |
| 3 | Show one changed sheet, its before/after quantity and its documented reason. | Film a 45–60 second quote comparison walkthrough with fictional inputs and normalized units. |
| 4 | Show the handoff from verified quantity to contractor rates, reviewed basis and bidder sign-off. | Invite one scoped pilot with a released package and confirmed bid date. |

The estimating lead clears accuracy and scope. Marketing clears permissions and post text. Track qualified project inquiries, paid pilots, repeat bidders, reviewer time and issues found before handoff.
