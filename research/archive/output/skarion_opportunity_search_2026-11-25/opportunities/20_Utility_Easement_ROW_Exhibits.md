1. Work from the Dec 1 Grand Junction and Dec 3 Calcasieu deadlines. Ask the named survey firms about bid intent, the ability to name a desktop sub, the surveyor who will own review, and the applicable data rules. Verify 04265 and its Nov 30 question deadline in parallel.
