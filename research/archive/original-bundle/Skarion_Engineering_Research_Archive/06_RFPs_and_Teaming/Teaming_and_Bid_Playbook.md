## Bid gate
1. Official solicitation and addenda; status, deadline/timezone, mandatory meeting, eligibility, staffing, insurance, registration, PE/survey license, certifications, field work, past references, security and offshore/subcontract permissions.
2. Deliverables, technical owner, QA, actual credentials, dependencies, cost to bid, rate/fee, capacity, delivery timeline and expected margin.
3. Decide Go / Conditional / No-Go; document blocker. Prepare tailored proposal only if viable. Authorized human approves bids, spending, pricing, scope, contractual commitments and submission.

Maintain one deduplicated pipeline with views for direct RFPs, teaming and private/freelance work. Preserve historical closed leads and reasons. Fields: unique ID, buyer, source/date, scope, location, budget (unknown if not published), deadline, eligibility, priority, owner, next step, status, proposal link, contact history and responses.
