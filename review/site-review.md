## Website review

- [ ] Sales lead confirms target buyers, offer language and the smallest paid scope.
- [ ] Technical reviewer confirms outputs, scope boundaries, prerequisites, format and acceptance checks.
- [ ] Operations checks capacity, qualified reviewers, turnaround language and data transfer.
- [ ] Content lead checks logo legibility, contrast, image rights, original copy and social crop.
- [ ] Web lead tests keyboard navigation, phone and desktop layouts, upstream links and a draft inquiry on the target domain.
- [ ] Owner confirms registrar, permanent URLs, approved intake implementation and go-live date.

## Portfolio review

Use the example galleries and sample downloads to review each named handoff. These examples are synthetic. Review any real client work separately in the private project queue and keep peer examples there as benchmarks. For each proposed client case, capture Skarion's actual role, project owner, inputs, outputs, date, approvals, redactions, visual rights and technical sign-off.

- [ ] A reviewer opens each sample file and reconciles record IDs, quantities, units, revisions and stated assumptions with the example page.
- [ ] The offer lead confirms the inputs, formats, quality checks and scopes on the capabilities page reflect the work the team can accept.
- [ ] The content lead checks that illustrative work, third-party research and approved client evidence are correctly identified before public release.

The product of the first round is one approved draft page for each active offer and a documented scope decision for each pilot. Keep a separate sign-off for a finished case study or a social campaign.

## Research behind the drafts

The November 26, 2026 Skarion benchmark compared 336 company entries across six lanes, 120 project references and 77 public social links. Companies can appear in more than one lane. It covered independent service firms, engineering primes and relevant adjacent providers. The portfolio review briefs cite the cases used to shape each site and its proposed sample. The counts describe the curated benchmark.
