# Shared outbound GTM engine
One CRM and data policy, niche-specific prospect criteria and approved portfolios. Suggested automation target 80–90% of repetitive research/admin work, not measured performance. Human mandatory involvement is driven by risk and commitments.

| Workflow | AI role | Human role |
|---|---|---|
| Company discovery / research / enrichment | Find public signals, source URLs, deduplicate and score | Audit evidence and approve buyer criteria |
| Outreach preparation | Choose approved portfolio, personalize drafts | Approve campaign claims, templates and sampled messages |
| Routine email and follow-ups | Approved sequence only, suppress opt-outs, stop on reply | Monitor compliance, deliverability and exceptions; explicit approval for sending until authorized |
| Replies / meeting booking | Classify, prepare draft, schedule when authorized | Handle sensitive/positive replies and discovery |
| Scoping / pricing / proposal | Draft estimates and checklists | Specialty expert and commercial owner approve scope, costs and dates |
| Contract / bid / regulated engineering | Support documentation | Authorized signatory and qualified professionals decide, approve and review |

LinkedIn: human-operated messages and connection requests; no unauthorized bots/scraping. US commercial email: accurate sender/subject, postal address, opt-out, suppression and compliance review (FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business). Separate UK/EU compliance assessment before campaigns. No unauthorized external sending. Use only licensed data/software and approved client permissions.

## 14-day validation
Days 1–2: one capability sheet, three samples, define approved scope. Days 3–5: 20 qualified accounts per top five niches (100 total). Days 6–10: 10–15 manually reviewed first-contact drafts per lane, calls, disqualifiers. Days 11–14: paid pilot offers, reviewer roster, compare verified replies, pilot interest, contribution margin and technical risk. No headcount expansion without evidence.

## Cadence (Tuesday–Sunday)
| Day | Focus | Small measurable goal |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Drafting | Five qualified accounts, two personalized introductions |
| Wednesday | Lighting | Five prospects and one technically accurate pitch |
| Thursday | GIS | Five utility/municipal or prime prospects, one draft |
| Friday | Stormwater | Five licensed partner targets; PE/insurance/subcontract checks |
| Saturday | GTM | CRM hygiene, two follow-up drafts, train/cross-train specialists |
| Sunday | CEO review | Actual leads/replies/calls/proposals/pilots, spend, consultant needs, ONE next priority |
Work block: 60–90 minutes, or 15 minutes on a busy day to delegate one action. Never mark outreach as sent unless actually sent. Unknown metrics stay unknown. Keep original task schedules separate from this archival document.

## CRM fields
Account, specialty, target buyer role, official source and date, need signal, service fit, jurisdiction, contact permission, owner, stage, last contact, next action, opt-out, proposal, actual spend, paid-pilot status, delivery hours/rework/margin. Stages: new → qualified → contacted → replied → discovery → scoped → paid pilot → repeat / closed. Review weekly with actual counts, not estimates.
