## Tests for Skarion

- **Interview electrical contractors with a two-layout mockup. Hook: “Same lot. Same criteria. What changes with the fixture?” Keep geometry, grid and criteria fixed. Show spacing, mounting height and IES assumptions. Use calculated values only after the reviewer clears the model**. Measure buyers who can state their acceptance criteria and share a real fixture cut sheet or layout request.
