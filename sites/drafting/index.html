Discuss the first batch

  1. Define the inputs

    Agree on approved redlines, base files, CAD version, plot standards, sheet list, revision allowance and deadline.

  2. Draft and check

    Incorporate the instructions, check layers and plots, and flag ambiguities for your reviewer.

  3. Hand off for review

    Issue the CAD files, plotted sheets and exception log so your team can accept the set or direct revisions.

Before you send drawings

Can you work with our CAD standards?

Share your template, layer and plot conventions, required software version and reference-file expectations. We confirm what the batch can follow before quoting.

What if the redlines conflict or a base file is missing?

We log conflicting or incomplete instructions against the source sheet for your reviewer. If an editable base is unavailable, we can scope a redraw from legible material.

Who is responsible for survey, design and approval?

Your designated reviewer or accountable professional handles field verification, survey determinations, design interpretation and any required approval or sealing. Skarion follows the approved drafting instructions.

How should we share project files?

Start with a short brief and your deadline. We can agree on a file transfer process, access restrictions and any required NDA before you share drawings.
