# Skarion RFP and Prime Contractor Teaming Report

United States · November 25, 2026 · Scope covers all 25 handover niches

## Where to start

The original market study used marketplace postings for some supplier and pricing observations. Its purchase evidence also came from public RFPs, bid tabs, council approvals, task orders, and named prime/subconsultant workshares. This follow-up uses **public procurement and direct prime evidence** to identify what Skarion could pursue. Every niche below links to its full original market brief, including the underlying buyer and competitor evidence.

The best near-term public pursuits for Skarion's production model are **Baltimore County fiber through a construction prime**, **utility GIS through Marina Coast or established wet-utility integrators**, and **Cherokee Nation lead service line records through an eligible team**. **Massport A460** and **Traverse City stormwater** are good fits for discrete subconsultant scopes if a qualified prime is already assembling its team. **Otsego** is a close technical fit with a November 27 deadline, so the practical route is through a team that already has the package or through the awardee after selection.

An owner advertising a prime contract provides a teaming trigger. A documented award identifies the firm already doing the work. A published supplier-intake page identifies a real onboarding route. A funded subcontract requires confirmation from the prime, and sometimes owner approval. For Skarion’s hybrid delivery model, confirm personnel location, data access and processing terms, software and owner approval before offering any delivery dates or prices.

### 01 Drafting and CAD overflow

**Public purchase evidence.** East Bay Municipal Utility District previously solicited as-needed CAD production because in-house teams faced peak workload. The 2022 board package names proposed consultant and subconsultant workshares and sought up to $2 million across five firms including the optional year. USACE Portland Hydroelectric Design Center documents repeat conversion calls. Massport A460 is the best current mixed CAD and facility records route. Its reality-capture and engineering scope calls for a multidisciplinary prime. [EBMUD scope](https://www.ebmud.com/application/files/1316/3884/1738/2021_As-needed_CAD_RFP.pdf) · [EBMUD board record](https://www.ebmud.com/application/files/1916/4972/0070/041222_Regular_Meeting_Staff_Reports.pdf) · [USACE procurement](https://sam.gov/opp/0593648e69ca42dbb43cae5f70f5fd91/view) · [A460](https://www.commbuys.com/bso/external/bidDetail.sda?docId=BD-19-1411-CAPPR-LOC1-134446)

**Prime route.** Q-CAD is a documented federal conversion incumbent and a potential overflow or competitor benchmark. PS&S is an A/E supplier-intake route. For civil and utility drafting, target a CAD or project delivery manager who owns a queue of incoming revisions. On Massport, use the current briefing attendee list as a prospect list after confirming each firm's role. An attendee may be a vendor, partner, or prospective lead. [Q-CAD order record](https://govtribe.com/award/federal-idv-award/blanket-purchase-agreement-w9127n26aa002) · [PS&S prequalification](https://www.psands.com/vendor-supplier-prequalifications-application)

**Paid package and gate.** Convert one clean existing-condition source or reconcile three to five approved redline sheets. Deliver editable CAD, a plot set, layers and references checks, and issue log. Confirm supplied dimensions, revision authority, client templates, access and who accepts the result. Quote from the actual sheet count and complexity. Federal controlled drawings require explicit permission for the actual data and personnel locations. **Pursue as a defined production subcontract.** [Full niche report](research/01_Drafting_CAD_Overflow.md)
