## Research behind the replacements

- Contractor closeout. The NBU handoff requires CAD, GPS points, revised plan/profile information and contractor redlines. Fairfax County describes who submits covered as-built work and ties approval to final bond release. The utility sample uses source and issue versions and carries an explicit closeout exception. [New Braunfels Utilities](https://www.nbutexas.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Record-Drawing-CAD-GPS-Deliverables-Standards-7.22.2019.pdf), [Fairfax County](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/as-built-plan-coordination)
- Existing-conditions production. A buyer’s listing describes measured information and photographs as inputs and an editable CAD and PDF handoff. Portland’s commercial plan guidance gives examples of building plan information to document. The technical sample indexes simulated views and source dimensions. [AutoCAD drafting buyer listing](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/AutoCAD-drafting-built-drawings_~022118521493256367237/), [Portland commercial plan guidance](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/commercial-permitting/changing-commercial-space/prepare/create-detailed-plans)
- Civil revisions. The buyer listing describes drafting from engineer markups. The handoff separates the survey base, the revised geometry and decisions pending with the engineer. [Civil drafting buyer listing](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Civil-Drafter_~022123411983277513374/), [Contra Costa Water District drawing production manual](https://ccwater.com/DocumentCenter/View/282/CCWD-Drawing-Production-and-CADD-Manual-PDF?bidId=)

## CAD and plot review

The three complete packages contain AutoCAD 2018-format DWGs with foot-based editable model-space geometry and numbered paper layouts, matching 17 × 11 in vector PDFs, source packet and review records. The exported DWGs were reopened with ODA File Converter and compared with source CAD for model geometry, layer inventory, paper layout names, viewport settings and the civil host’s relative reference. The PDF sheets were rendered and visually reviewed. [ODA File Converter](https://www.opendesign.com/guestfiles/oda_file_converter)

Autodesk AutoCAD was unavailable for the review. A live delivery should use the recipient’s CAD version and plot standards. Replace fictional records with actual source material only after the rights holder clears the material for portfolio use.
