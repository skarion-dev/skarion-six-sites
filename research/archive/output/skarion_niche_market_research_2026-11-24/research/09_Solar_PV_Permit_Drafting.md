# 09 Solar PV permit drafting

**As of November 24, 2026.** US installers buy permit plan sets and recurring back-office permitting support. Standard residential PV sheets face visible global competition, low posted prices, and automated substitutes. Buyer-directed revisions on unusual retrofit jobs remain a plausible secondary offer with supplier availability unmeasured.

## Offer and buyers

Target residential solar and storage service contractors, regional installers, or specialist plan-set firms with repeat jobs in one permitting jurisdiction and utility territory. Offer an editable AutoCAD redline or a fixed-scope draft package from the buyer's approved layout, survey and photos, equipment schedule, approved one-line diagram, and drawing standards. Return a revision record and a short list of unresolved items for the buyer's reviewer. The installer or its licensed engineering partner confirms the design and owns calculations, seals where required, and submissions. The repeat trigger is an equipment swap, an existing-system retrofit, plan-review comments, or a weekly backlog. The City and County of Fairfax publish different requirements, so use the exact jurisdiction checklist. The City guide calls for a dated engineer's seal, and the County describes scope-dependent requirements and possible typical details. [City of Fairfax plan requirements](https://www.fairfaxva.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/permitting/documents/flowcharts-and-guides/solar-installations-plan-requirements-20250926.pdf) · [Fairfax County residential solar](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/permit-library/solar-residential)

## What could change the decision

The attractive validation question is whether an installer has a **paid queue of exception revisions** that its current drafting desk or automation cannot clear on schedule. A buyer could share three recent redacted files, the typical payment per revision, the required turnaround, and its reasons for using outside help. Compare the paid queue with the incumbent's next available turnaround and the buyer's QA standard. Verify how many packages the installer has actually purchased in the last month and how many it would offer to a second provider. A sample schedule and a queue are more informative than installation megawatts or a generic count of permits.

Start with one paid revision on an existing single-family retrofit, in one AHJ, using the approved equipment schedule and electrical design. Scope the marked-up set, editable DWG, PDF, one review cycle, and a document list. Price after timing production and senior review and accounting for rework. A $50–$100 selling price leaves $32.50–$65 for direct delivery cost at an illustrative 35% contribution threshold before acquisition and overhead. That makes price-first competition unattractive if each job requires substantial reviewer time. Confirm a named reviewer, buyer drawing access, revision authority, and signed-off baseline before quoting. Treat independent circuit sizing, new structural details, seal, permit filings, and utility follow-up as separate responsibilities.
