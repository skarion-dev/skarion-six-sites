# Skarion Engineering Expansion Review

November 24, 2026 · Internal decision brief

## Recommended expansion

Run **five GTM campaigns in parallel** through one shared research, outreach drafting, and CRM workflow. Test Drafting, Utility GIS, Lighting, Stormwater, and OSP closeout and utility records. Drafting has the strongest documented demand signal. OSP closeout and GIS draw most directly on Skarion’s existing work. Buyer responses and delivery readiness determine which pilots move forward.

Use one verified contracting entity and specialty-specific portfolios. Drafting and GIS can share production, project management, QA, and account relationships. Keep OSP as the commercial anchor. Give each campaign a narrow offer, a buyer trigger, and an approved sample or a discovery question that can stand on its own. Advance an opportunity to a priced pilot when it has a named producer, qualified review, and an agreed acceptance standard.

The next commercial milestone is **a paid, accepted pilot followed by a viable repeat-work quote in whichever campaign earns it first**. More than one pilot can run when reviewer and production capacity support it. Evidence of repeat orders, collected revenue, and actual job costs should drive any increase in fixed spending.

### First paid drafting pilot

Use the report’s **$149 starting test** for one uncomplicated, adequately dimensioned client-supplied plan. Return a clean native DWG, plotted PDF, a short assumption and exception log, and a documented QA check. Include one round of in-scope redlines. Quote after confirming source quality, CAD version, units, layers, title block, and reviewer availability. The report’s two-to-three-business-day target starts when complete inputs are accepted. Client approval controls the drawing’s use. Field measurement, design changes, code determinations, and certification require separate scoping.

For the infrastructure buyer, quote one small batch of client-marked record drawings separately. Reconcile each markup to the source sheet, preserve revision history, flag unresolved dimensions or conflicts, and offer a follow-on batch once the buyer accepts the standard. Build three authorized or original demonstration samples covering PDF-to-DWG, a redline set with an issue log, and an infrastructure as-built or CAD-to-GIS handoff.

The original report’s $22–$32 hourly CAD support range and $149 package are quote hypotheses. The later [drafting stress test](../drafting-business/stress_test_results.json) enumerates 4,228,560 combinations of assumed costs, pricing, capacity, and conversion. It supplies a sensitivity model. Its calibrated illustration estimates about $18.76 to deliver a small sample and $40.02 for a full pilot under one set of assumptions. Actual labor, utilization, review, revisions, and sales costs still determine the quote. For each pilot, capture both delivery contribution and payback after acquisition cost using collected revenue, actual production and review hours, PM, rework, software and transaction costs, and any subsidized entry work.

The later [low-price trial protocol](../drafting-business/low-price-trial-protocol.txt) describes an optional $0/$25/$49 test for a drawing excerpt or task with up to 1.5 production hours. Keep that small scope and its proposed caps if leadership runs the test. A subsidized pilot can serve as customer acquisition cost if tracked explicitly. Measure paid follow-ons, repeat purchases, and cumulative payback. The $149 full-sheet offer is the recommended first test because it asks directly for payment and sets up a measurable delivery standard.

The original 8-week training plan is useful for conversion, layers and plotting, dimensions, revisions, and handoff QA. Start with available staff and a named reviewer. Use a timed, reviewed sample with no critical omissions as the release gate. Staff millwork, Revit, and specialist Civil 3D work when the team can demonstrate proficiency and the required software seats. Upwork remains a selective channel. Its current rules require Agency Plus for most agency proposals and offers, with Enterprise exceptions. Check the live Skarion membership and shared Connects before committing spend.
