# Skarion Estimating website and social plan

**Pursue now.** Position this offer as a bid-desk extension for US fiber and civil utility contractors. Launch with plan-traceable takeoffs, bid schedules and quote leveling. Offer a priced bid package after a named estimator can review the contractor's local inputs and the contractor accepts the basis. The provisional display name is **Skarion Estimating**. Use the shared Skarion Engineering identity and buying intake while the team reviews the separate domain.

## Who buys it and what they get

| Buyer | Trigger | Starting deliverable | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| OSP contractors, ISP construction teams and design-build primes | A released aerial, underground or mixed build package, an imminent bid or an addendum | One route or package. Bid-item quantities, marked plans, bill of materials, units, revision log, assumptions and outstanding questions | The prime provides the approved design and construction criteria. Its estimator owns the construction method, rates and final bid. |
| Wet utility and heavy civil contractors | A Division 33 water, sewer or storm plan set, tie-in or phased municipal package | A worksheet for pipe by length and size, fittings, structures, depth bands, restoration, alternates and exclusions with sheet or station references | Field conditions, production decisions, shoring, dewatering and risk allowances stay with the responsible project team. |
| Prime estimating and procurement teams | Multiple supplier or trade quotes, items priced in inconsistent units or a late revision | An aligned comparison, normalization notes, scope gaps, dated sources and a revision delta | Keep confidential quotes in an approved project workspace. The buyer confirms awards and final terms. |

### Services and packaging

1. **Quantity takeoff and bid schedule.** Fixed scope by clean plan set or defined work package after scoping. Include a native editable workbook, marked PDF, sheet and addendum references, assumptions and exclusions, and one agreed clarification cycle. Show the separation between measured quantities, allowances and unanswered RFIs.
2. **Bid revision and quote leveling.** Review changed sheets, issue a measurable delta and reconcile supplier quotes to common codes and units. Record validity, freight, lead-time assumptions and exclusions where supplied. A scoped recurring arrangement can cover repeat bidders after a paid pilot establishes turnaround and revision effort.
3. **Estimator-reviewed pricing.** Launch after an experienced construction estimator is assigned and the bidder provides approved rates, supplier and subcontractor quotes, wage/burden, equipment and productivity, scheduling/access, risk and markup inputs. Return a basis-of-estimate with independent reviewer notes, dated rate sources, exclusions and escalation. The client's authorized estimator or officer owns the bid price and submission. Review needed credentials and contract limits with the prime before quoting.

### Acceptance checklist

- Authorized plan revision and bid schedule agree with the intake record. Files are permitted for the team's location, access and retention.
- Units, takeoff symbol legend, limits of work and alternates are stated. Every important quantity traces to a sheet, station, feature or approved instruction.
- Independent spot-check reconciles pipe and conduit lengths, structure and fixture counts, sums, references and changed sheets. Differences against owner prefilled quantities are explained.
- Ambiguities and allowances are called out. Licensed design decisions and field verifications have a named owner. A reviewer approves separately priced labor, equipment, subcontract quotes and productivity.
- Demonstration pages use original diagrams. Third-party case links remain labeled outside examples or are cited for internal review.

## Manual launch sequence

1. **Week 1.** Assign an offer lead, independent estimating reviewer, OSP technical lead, civil reviewer and publisher. Decide core scope, terminology, sample authorization and rate-review availability. Draft the homepage, two service pages and intake.
2. **Week 2.** Build and independently check the first two synthetic examples. Test bid date, time-zone, version and confidentiality fields. Route fiber and civil intake to one shared tracker with lane, stage and project owner.
3. **Week 3.** Review the proposal path with someone who bids US construction work. Confirm how bidder-provided rates, supplier quotes, crew productivity, trade coverage and exclusions enter the priced workflow. Add the revision sample and record reviewer approval.
4. **Week 4.** Review initial prospect conversations and publish approved examples only after team sign-off. Record qualified bid sets, paid pilot requests, repeat work, review effort and errors caught before handoff. Use those observations to decide whether to expand estimating capacity and launch the priced tier.
