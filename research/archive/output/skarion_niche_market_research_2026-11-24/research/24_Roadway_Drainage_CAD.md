# Roadway drainage CAD

**Reachable demand 3/5, medium confidence. Supply tightness 3/5 for bundled engineering primes, medium confidence. Supply of qualified production subcontractors for this exact scope is unknown. Accessibility is medium through an approved prime with a defined work package.** Public owners repeatedly purchase roadway drainage design and associated plans. A Texas letting set shows an outside specialist producing the actual sheets, but published price and competition evidence mostly covers complete engineering engagements.

## Offer and buyer

Turn an approved alignment, surface, storm sewer or culvert design, and engineer redlines into native plan and profile sheets, cross sections, structure labels, and a bid-item quantity summary. The buyer is a transportation engineering prime, a lead drainage subconsultant, or a civil consultant on a county or city roadway project. The repeat triggers are corridor task orders, 30/60/90/final reviews, recurring small culvert replacements, and letting deadlines. The prime’s engineer of record owns sizing, inlet and outfall decisions, hydraulic and hydrologic analysis, quantity approval, and final issue. This scope is distinct from a watershed or stormwater master plan and from documenting the condition or coordinates of existing culverts.

## Decision and test

Keep roadway drainage CAD in the second wave. The TxDOT set provides meaningful proof of subcontracted delivery, and KCI separately states it manages hydraulic and roadway design subcontractors for another TxDOT corridor. Taken together with named KYTC and NYSDOT incumbents, there are specific firms to qualify. The subcontract split and available supplier capacity at the proposed desktop scope remain unmeasured. Count repeat work only when a prime identifies actual funded projects or regular authorized milestones, and confirm whether the prime may add a production subcontractor. [KCI US 281 workshare](https://www.kci.com/projects/us-281-improvements/) · [TxDOT consultant precertification](https://www.txdot.gov/business/peps/become-precertified.html)

Offer one paid three-sheet trial for a single drainage segment or culvert, using prime-approved design inputs. Return the client's native model/drawings, plotted PDFs, a quantity cross-check against approved inputs, and a tracked revision log. Confirm Bentley OpenRoads or the specified native version, DGN seed, coordinate system, workspace and access, dependent references, and actual reviewer capacity before quoting. Some DOT environments require named external consultant access and specific file naming or workspace conventions. Review the subcontract's staff and data-access terms for the actual delivery location. A useful comparison is hours and iterations until the prime accepts a complete submittal. Ask the prime how many approved vendors quote or turn around the same native package and the size of its next recurring batch.
