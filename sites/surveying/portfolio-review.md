# Skarion Survey CAD portfolio review

Start with the utility corridor case for the paid pilot. Use point processing, profiles and as-built comparison for related work. The residential case remains a practice sample for client-supplied geometry and the Long Island benchmark. Each case uses fictional coordinates and a review draft.

| Example | Evidence | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SV-501 utility corridor | 16 RTK, locator, owner-record and GPR markup entries in a layered drawing | Trace four approved locator ties. Keep the water sketch record-only. Leave the GPR response’s identity and depth open as S-05. |
| SV-401 residential boundary and topo | Supplied parcel, ground points and features, a contour sheet and S-04 | Match the 100 × 130 ft boundary and mask contours under the house and pool. Carry the observed fence position without making a legal determination. |
| SV-101 coded topographic draft | Twelve point records, assigned feature lines, a vector plot and S-01 | Preserve 102.24 ft at point 105. Check that point 112 remains a review item. |
| SV-201 existing-ground profile | Five accepted stations with an open sixth station | Match 103.15 ft at 0+60. Stop the ground line at 1+20 and keep 1+50 on S-02. |
| SV-301 as-built asset record comparison | Earlier and observed positions and S-03 | Resolve MH-02 as +3.00 ft easting. Leave MH-03’s unreported invert blank. |

The website files use editable DXF and vector PDF. These are illustrative drawing exports. Agree the production CAD software, version, survey settings and final file format during scoping. Confirm the agreed file opens and the source parameters survive import.
