## Work package and buyer

Sell **native AutoCAD production for fleet or commercial EV charging sites** to engineering teams that already own the design. Use their survey or base DWG, charger locations, equipment schedules, routing markups, one-line content, approved details, CAD standards, and templates. Deliver coordinated site and equipment sheets, a drafted single-line diagram or detail sheet where specified, a checklist of unresolved input conflicts, and a revision log with DWG, reference files, and plotted PDF. The design owner supplies or approves electrical capacity, conductor and equipment selections, utility coordination, accessibility and civil decisions, required certifications, and issuance. The closest US marketplace buyer describes reusable site templates, existing drawing references, and technical direction from its engineering team. [Direct CAD buyer](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/AutoCAD-Civil-site-plan-design-drafter_~022107231290235737861/)

Target design managers at multidisciplinary A&E firms, design-build EV charging contractors, and regional electrical installers with a named design lead and several permitted or funded sites in their pipeline. The permit burden varies by project and jurisdiction. San Diego, for example, publishes a no-plan permit route for eligible residential private garages, while its commercial and multifamily guidance calls for site and electrical plans and specifies who can sign and stamp the electrical plans and calculations. Start with one jurisdiction and an existing customer standard. Widen the geography after the buyer confirms its approval coverage and staffing rules. [San Diego permit bulletin 187](https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/dsdib187.pdf)

## Ranking and first sales test

**Second wave unless a prime confirms a recurring backlog it will pay to subcontract.** The strongest route is a small production package within a multi-site US fleet program. Generic permit drafting has visible low-priced supply and only one closely matched US marketplace buyer was found. Public A&E demand is well documented, while the small sample, licensing filters, and unobserved subcontract budgets limit conclusions about scarcity for Skarion’s actual offer. Account targets can overlap with utility CAD, solar, and MEP. Count a single mixed project once and assign it to the offer the buyer intends to purchase.

Ask an established installation or design firm for its next 60 days of approved site drawings, mean revision turnaround, frequency of plan-check changes, and policy on third-party or offshore drafting. Seek evidence of prior paid outsourcing or explicit intention to fund a trial. Require a confirmed design owner and verify permitted staffing geography, CAD version, standards, file access, source backgrounds, and approval responsibilities. Offer **one paid site milestone** covering redline incorporation on up to three existing sheets, with one revision allowance, an exceptions checklist, native DWGs, packaged references, and matched PDFs. Quote after reviewing the inputs and benchmark in-house production hours, review cycles, acceptance, and the next awardable batch. A paid extension to two or more subsequent sites would be stronger evidence of commercial fit than the public charger count.
