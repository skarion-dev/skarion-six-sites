# 10 Lead service line inventory records and GIS

**Research cutoff November 24, 2026 · United States**

The November 2027 inventory deadline and recurring updates are already producing contracts that include tap-card digitization, records reconciliation, and GIS updates. Public records show a feasible subcontracting route and a few thin prime competitions, with mixed competition overall and no reliable count of qualified suppliers for the remote production package.

| Measure | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Reachable demand | **4/5, medium-high confidence.** Multiple utilities have purchased inventory work, and named buyer records show a separable records package and an outsourced production component. The current volume of unfilled subcontract packages is unknown. |
| Supply tightness | **3/5, medium confidence for bundled professional primes. Unknown for scoped remote records/GIS support.** Observed prime counts include 2 and 6 in separate cities and 5 after a Trenton rebid. Geographic and eligibility differences limit the comparison. |
| Access for Skarion | **Medium via an authorized prime or data-services buyer.** Verify similar references, approved client environment, data handling and production locations, business registration, and any subcontract disclosure before pricing. Physical records, field verification, complete regulatory submissions, and software-platform commitments raise the bar. |
| Top-five decision | Include in the **parallel validation shortlist**. Start with evidence-linked records reconciliation for existing utility consultants. Make demand urgency and separable delivery the case for testing, with supplier availability explicitly unmeasured. |

## Offer and reachable buyers

Sell a fixed batch of **tap-card and work-order reconciliation with GIS-ready output** to a water engineering or inventory prime with an active records backlog. Extract documented material, connector and date evidence, match it to a stable service identifier, link records to source pages, normalize to the buyer's schema, and hand off unknowns, conflicts, overlaps and unmatched addresses for review. The utility or authorized prime decides the accepted material classification, field and validation strategy, and filings. Use approved digital copies in the client-approved environment. ArcGIS's editable inventory supports separate utility-side and customer-side information and the buyer can retain its existing system. [EPA records guidance](https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/planning-and-developing-service-line-inventory) · [Esri inventory workflow](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-solutions/latest/reference/use-lead-service-line-inventory.htm)

The 2021 revisions required an initial inventory by **October 16, 2024**. EPA's 2026 guidance puts the updated baseline under the Lead and Copper Rule Improvements on **November 1, 2027** for covered community and non-transient non-community systems. Existing records inform added connector details and updated line materials and locations. The first annual update under the new rule is **January 30, 2029**, with differences for all-non-lead systems. Later findings, repairs and replacements can create follow-on work. EPA's funding overview includes inventory options. A mandate, reported unknowns, or grant allocation alone does not establish an outsourced budget. [EPA August 2026 Inventory Tips](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-08/508_0813inventorytips_clean.pdf) · [EPA Region 8 inventory schedule](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-04/r8_lslr_policy-v0_20251215.pdf) · [Applicability FAQ](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-10/final_lcri_faq_statespws.pdf) · [EPA funding overview](https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/identifying-federal-funding-sources-lead-service-line-replacement)

## Pilot decision

Start with a prime that has signed work or authorized a records backlog. Hazen in Virginia and the Northeast engineering primes in the evidence table are examples of purchaser or teaming channels to qualify. EPA's reporting dashboard exposes system-level counts and inventory status, with differences in reporting completeness. Join those signals to utility procurement awards and public inventory project pages before treating a system as a funded buyer. Use one account record for primes also targeted in water/wastewater GIS and pitch the specific service-line evidence backlog. [EPA system-level dashboard](https://sdwis.epa.gov/ords/sfdw_pub/r/sfdw/sdwis_fed_reports_public/service-line-inventory)

Propose a fixed pilot of **300 supplied digital scans** in one agreed schema. Deliver a traceable crosswalk, evidence extraction and confidence annotations, and exceptions including uncertain ownership-side or connector evidence, duplicates and unmatched addresses. The prime supplies approved input and field interpretation conventions, controls material classifications and ArcGIS acceptance, and holds the owner relationship. Time both straight-through and ambiguous records, keep ordinary ingestion separate from exceptions, and quote follow-on batches against verified throughput and review effort. Pricing in the ledger is context for each buyer's package. St. Louis's two-rate structure is a relevant precedent for quote units. [St. Louis labor bid](https://www.stlwater.com/contact/24RFP019_Data_Entry_for_LSLI.pdf)

Verify the team's ArcGIS export, source audit and reviewer coverage using a scrubbed sample. Secure buyer-approved digital access, subcontract terms, staff location and reference eligibility for the specific route. The most valuable evidence after the desk research is a prime naming a **funded, separable batch**, its due date, the number of acceptable subcontract options, and what has prevented those options from clearing the backlog. Three independent buyers providing this detail would distinguish thin supplier availability from high buyer interest alone.
