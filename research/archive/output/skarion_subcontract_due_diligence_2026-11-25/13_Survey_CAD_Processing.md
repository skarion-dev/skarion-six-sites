## First steps

1. Ask Park and PCiRoads estimating about the Dec 1 packages first. Use the exact contract and pay item, state that the offer depends on prime-approved field capture, and ask which files, templates and surveyor approvals are required. MnDOT’s general as-built guidance distinguishes electronic asset records and plan redlines. Get the contract-specific format before quoting native CAD. [MnDOT general as-built guidance](https://dot.mn.gov/pre-letting/scene/docs/201809%20DESIGN%20SCENE%20Newsletter.pdf)
2. Route Grand Junction through the listed attendees before the owner’s Dec 1 cutoff. Qualify the proposed country of performance and a reviewed production unit before requesting a role in the proposal.
3. Call Repset for its current contractor route. Ask LKC for a separately priced drawing or surface batch only after prime execution and an approved field handoff. A useful small unit is one approved point file and redlined base, returned with a native Civil 3D drawing, approved surface if called for, retained point references and an issues log.
