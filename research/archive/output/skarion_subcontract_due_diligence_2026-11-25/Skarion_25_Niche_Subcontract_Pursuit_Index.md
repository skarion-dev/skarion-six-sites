## How to prioritize a lead

- **Direct** means a firm published a project-specific request for subcontractor pricing or qualifications. Check whether its advertised scope can be delivered separately and confirm the certification, licensing, delivery location, and data requirements.
- **Contingent** means a prospective prime published a quote intake that depends on its eligibility, decision to bid, and award. Ask who is preparing the proposal and who can accept the workshare.
- **Open intake** means the contractor publicly invites people or firms to apply for the service. The project and cutoff may be unpublished.
- **Named project** means a public record connects the firm to the work. Confirm that the relevant package is still available and that the firm and owner will approve a subcontract.
- **Bid team prospect** means a firm attended, held a prior contract, or has another project-specific connection. Confirm whether it is actually bidding. Project intake events provide a route to the procurement team.
- **Verify** means the date, current status, exact workshare, or another pursuit condition is unresolved. The linked brief explains the check.
