# Skarion Engineering’s 25-niche opportunity search

**US public procurement and prime-contractor teaming · November 25, 2026**

Twenty-five dedicated agents searched one niche each from the expansion handover. The briefs cover current owner solicitations, requests issued by primes, named awarded firms, and contracts to watch. Evidence comes from solicitation packages, addenda, award records, and prime-authored notices. The search excludes Upwork and job postings.

For the portfolio comparison of demand and supplier competition, see the [earlier niche market ranking](../skarion_niche_market_research_2026-11-24/Skarion_Niche_Market_Ranking.md).

## How to use the results

- **Direct quote request** means a prime invited quotes. Check that the trade scope and certification match Skarion before treating the invitation as actionable.
- **Open buyer solicitation** means the owner is accepting proposals. For a teaming lead, first identify a qualified bidder and verify its interest, the required subcontract disclosure, and the proposal deadline.
- **Awarded prime lead** identifies the firm holding an owner-approved scope. Ask whether the relevant batch is still available, may be subcontracted, and has a separate budget.
- **Watch or verify** means a decision, amendment, partner, eligibility condition, or precise production scope needs confirmation. Deadlines below reflect documents available at the time of search. January dates refer to 2027. Several briefs refer to the same procurement when it contains distinct work packages.
