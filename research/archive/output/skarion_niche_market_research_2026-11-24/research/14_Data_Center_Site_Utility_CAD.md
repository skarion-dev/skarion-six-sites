# Data center site utility CAD

Research cutoff November 24, 2026

**Reachable demand 3/5, medium confidence. Supplier tightness unknown for client-directed production. Skarion access low to medium through an approved civil, utility, or trade prime.** Municipal records show funded, recurring data-center utility engineering, and specialist case studies show subcontracting of underground coordination and drawings. The matched subcontract records disclose neither vendor competition nor fees, while engineering primes and trade contractors can supply or self-perform related work.

## Reachable offer

Deliver one client-directed utility drawing or record package for a campus phase, work zone, or off-site connection. Use approved design geometry, field-marked changes, and the buyer’s native drawing templates. Produce updated plan and profile sheets, referenced base files, a utility-crossing or coordination exception log, and a change register keyed to the buyer’s approved records. A second package can follow when a new phase, revised field survey, change order, or record-drawing handoff creates a repeatable batch.

The likely buyer is the civil engineer of record, an underground utility or electrical trade contractor, or a general contractor’s virtual design and construction (VDC) lead. Start with firms already assigned to a named campus or corridor, especially in Northern Virginia, where public planning records and a supplier case study identify relevant activity. Count an opening as reachable when a buyer can identify the drawing package, who owns technical approval, the subcontract route, and acceptable personnel and data access. Campus owner hiring, capital cost, or megawatts alone establish no CAD purchase. **Tightness call.** Approved city task orders went to firms with existing utility knowledge. That suggests a relationship-based sales channel. It does not measure qualified supply. Named specialist substitutes and in-house production are visible. Public award and private supplier sources sampled here did not show a bid tab, proposal count, or comparable fee for a stand-alone data center site utility CAD or as-built subcontract. Assign **Unknown**, with high confidence that the observed construction counts and in-house recruitment do not settle the narrower question.

## Commercial decision and test

**Keep as a targeted second-wave offer.** Pursue it sooner if an existing consultant or trade buyer can sponsor access and has a defined backlog. A local utility coordinator or VDC-enabled trade contractor has a closer relationship to the production package than a cold hyperscaler approach. Project counterparties can be identified from launches and approvals. For example, Vantage identifies McCarthy, Turner, Michels and other partners at Lighthouse, and McCarthy and Turner publish subcontract channels. Their public pages do not establish that Skarion is prequalified or that any particular lot is open to remote delivery. [Lighthouse partner announcement](https://blog.vantage-dc.com/2026/03/30/vantage-data-centers-and-partners-host-career-expo-in-port-washington-wisconsin-to-connect-local-talent-with-lighthouse-opportunities/) · [McCarthy trade partners](https://www.mccarthy.com/trade-partners) · [Turner prequalification](https://www.turnerconstruction.com/subcontractors)

Offer a paid two- or three-sheet pilot for one zone of owner-approved redlines. Return updated native Civil 3D or DWG files, PDF review sheets, and a traceable field-change and exception log. During scoping, confirm required software version, coordinate reference and model origin, external references and templates, ownership of source files, approved hosting and personnel access, and qualified sign-off. The buyer or its engineer confirms alignment and design decisions, review acceptance, and source survey or as-installed geometry. Price from the actual production and reviewer effort. A field survey, reroute design, electrical studies, and sealed documentation are distinct scopes.

The decision-changing evidence is **a buyer-confirmed repeat batch with a comparable history of supplier lead times, qualified quotes, or an unfilled deadline**. Ask the prime who produces the native sheets today, what they recently placed with a vendor, how many qualified vendors were available, and whether the information can be shared with an approved remote production team. Score it higher only after that specific evidence supports both subcontract access and a supply gap.
