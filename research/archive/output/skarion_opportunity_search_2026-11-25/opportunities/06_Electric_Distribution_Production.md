# 06 · Electric distribution drafting, records and utility GIS QA

**US opportunity check · November 25, 2026.** Start with WSP's recent Garland electric GIS award, then DGR’s municipal electric practice and the two JEA distribution engineering awardees. The strongest offer is a defined batch of owner-approved GIS exception cleanup or production drawings from prime-supplied design and field redlines. This search found an open construction-team prospect in Oregon and several funded prime contracts. It found no live standalone utility drafting bid or prime-issued subcontract quote request for that work.

**Highest priority · WSP on Garland REQ00002321.** Funding and buyer selection are documented at $2,199,464.35 and by the November 2 consent action. WSP is named. The RFP includes GIS data readiness and optional cleanup. WSP’s need to outsource, the size and funding of any cleanup work order, the current project phase, and eligible delivery locations remain unverified. WSP publishes a supplier registration route triggered by selection to team. The first step is to reach the GP&L delivery lead or WSP's Power & Energy utility GIS practice with a project-specific production offer. ## Pursuit sequence

1. Prepare one utility GIS QA sample and one field-redline-to-record CAD sample from synthetic assets. Show the input, exception IDs, documented edit or escalation, and independent review. Ask WSP which Garland readiness or optional cleanup batch remains unassigned, then carry the same offer to DGR with Fairmont Task Order 10 or a current municipal equivalent.
2. Approach Pickett and Chen Moore for the next JEA task with governed production work. Ask 1898 & Co. and RAMTeCH where municipal data correction or independent sampling may sit outside their base team. A quote should follow confirmation of the named task, volume, acceptance checks, pricing unit, reviewer, and approved workspace and work location.
3. Check the Northern Wasco package before the December 2 mandatory bidder meeting and use the amended requirements. Continue only if a qualified constructor shows a defined closeout package and its timing allows a sub. Watch Ocala’s replacement solicitation and the award of Garland's related software RFP.

Prime-level competition varies. JEA's 2025 packet records nine responses, Roseville’s procurement packet records 11, and Naperville’s records one. These counts describe competition for broad owner contracts. The public documents do not measure the number of suppliers available for desktop production, which is best tested through current batch volume, turnaround or price friction, and willingness to place paid work.
