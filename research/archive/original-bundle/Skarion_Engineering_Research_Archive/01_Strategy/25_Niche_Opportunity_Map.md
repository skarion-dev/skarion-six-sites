## Prioritization
Immediate discovery: CAD overflow, GIS, SUE records, water/wastewater GIS, as-built digitization, utility permitting, OSP closeout. Partner-led pilots: lighting, stormwater, electric distribution, Civil 3D, solar, lead service lines, pole attachment, survey CAD, culverts and erosion control. Later: traffic, EV, data-center utilities, floodplain, roadway drainage, BIM, ROW/easements, expanded OSP depending on capacity. These are strategic hypotheses rather than measured demand.
