## Procurement checks for the open opportunities

**Erie.** Submit one signed original and five copies to the county in Buffalo, with separately sealed cost proposals. The RFP lists no pre-proposal conference. I found no express subcontracting, offshore-access or geographic-delivery language in the publicly posted RFP text. Confirm with the prime and in the county’s written question process before budgeting a delivery location, data access or a subcontract. Do so by noon ET December 14. The county confirms the stated scope and open procurement. A public budget amount, named bidders and a purchasable subcontract remain unverified. [County RFP](https://www3.erie.gov/purchasing/sites/www3.erie.gov.purchasing/files/2026-11/tax-mapping-rfp-final-version-rfp-2026-057vf.pdf).

**MarinMap.** The RFP permits Bid Express, designated email or a physical package and asks for subcontractor roles, relevant experience and resumes. Sample contract §8 allows work identified in the agreement and requires prior written county authorization for other subcontract work. Subcontractors must carry the same coverages as the prime and name both the prime and the county as additional insureds on general liability. Appendix A covers ArcGIS Online, hosted data and transition work, and permits optional professional services through separately authorized task orders. A production block can be proposed within those services. Actual funding for that block, participation by any particular prime, and worker location/data access eligibility require confirmation. [RFP instructions and appendix](https://assets.marincounty.gov/marincounty-prod/public/2026-11/RFP%202910%20-%20Managed%20GIS%20Services%20for%20Marinmap.pdf?VersionId=jm3vq6PO_8eihKTpBugCr.hWYc_HWZTb), [Bid Express agency page](https://www.bidexpress.com/businesses/53528/home).

## Pursuit sequence

1. Prepare a small public-data ArcGIS Pro sample with before-and-after parcel annotation, topology checks, field mapping, a discrepancy log and a prime sign-off sheet. Offer a fixed first batch, then quote recurring throughput once the prime can specify input quality, QA rules and records per batch.
2. Qualify potential MarinMap teams before the December 8 question cutoff and Erie teams before December 14. Ask whether they are bidding, whether the QA package fits, who reviews it and what contract or data restrictions govern delivery. Have the prime include the work in its proposal when appropriate.
3. Follow up separately on existing contracts with Kosmos and Draw TAP. For Kosmos, confirm amendment execution and contract-administrator approval before treating the proposed expansion as available work. For Draw TAP, ask about upcoming map layers and whether approved funding covers the package.
4. Track the Missoula and Biddeford award records instead of carrying their closed tenders as live leads. Procurement notices and municipal awards demonstrate that buyers commission the work. This targeted sample lacks comparable bid counts and visibility into prime vendor rosters, so it cannot establish a low-supply market.

**Scope.** This brief covers general GIS, parcels, land use and boundaries. Water and wastewater utility asset conversion belongs to niche 08. Searches used official solicitations, agency meeting records and prime service pages. No outreach was sent.
