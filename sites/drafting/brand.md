# Skarion Drafting brand kit

Working identity for the CAD drafting and as-builts practice. The buyer is a contractor, facility records lead, architect or engineering production manager with a defined drafting scope, authorized source records and a deadline.

## Positioning and voice

- **Headline.** From field redlines to record drawings.
- **What we sell.** A defined drafting batch from approved client inputs. The closeout handoff includes editable native drawings, plotted sheets, a drawing index and an exception log.
- **First engagement.** Scope one to three representative sheets. Confirm whether files open as expected, check the template and plots with the buyer’s reviewer, and use the findings to quote follow-on batches.
- **Voice.** Practical, precise and calm. Name the file, drawing convention, input or review decision. Prefer “log the unresolved tie-in against sheet U-01” over general assurances about accuracy or speed.
- **Buyer responsibility.** The customer designates the accountable reviewer for field verification, design interpretation, survey determinations and any required approval or sealing.

## Logo and artwork

The mark folds the corner of a plot sheet over a redline. The mark is [logo.svg](logo.svg). Pair with the words **Skarion Drafting**, set as on the website. Use the [light-plate mark](brand-assets/mark-on-dark.svg) on dark surfaces when the standard plate would lose edge definition. Keep a clear space of one-quarter of the mark width around the composition. Use the icon alone at 24 px and above, the paired lockup at 110 px and above, and the icon on a square centered crop for avatars. Keep the plan sheet and redline in their original proportions.

**Type.** Georgia for editorial headlines. System Inter where installed, then Arial for navigation, copy and forms. Consolas or the system monospace for plan labels. Keep body copy at 16 px or larger, text inputs at 16 px, and line height near 1.6. Export social artwork to PNG so recipients see the intended fonts.

**Art direction.** Lead with deliverables the buyer can inspect. Draw a bounded scene, use quiet gridlines, and highlight a single discrepancy or resolution. Make legends readable and label invented drawings as illustrative. Avoid client logos, job file screenshots and as-built measurements unless the reviewer and rights owner approve the exact material.

Use plots from the relevant technical sample. Give screenshots a caption that names the change and any open item.

## Social and launch

Start on the approved Skarion LinkedIn business presence. Use two posts per week for four weeks. Technical owner reviews before posting. Capture qualified inquiries, first-batch briefs, paid starts, hours of rework, accepted handoffs and second-batch requests.

| Week | Post and asset | Buyer action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “What to send before your as-built deadline” checklist. Then show one original ambiguous note and a matching exception entry. | Read the record drawing scope. |
| 2 | “From three markups to a checked record set” carousel. Then explain what an index tells the reviewer. | Open the approved utility sample when available. |
| 3 | “When an old plan needs a scale check” diagram. Then show a brief, approved layer cleanup walkthrough. | Scope a legacy redraw. |
| 4 | “How a reviewer checks units and coordinates” checklist. Then share what a first batch reveals about recurring CAD work. | Discuss a drawing batch. |

**Domain.** Proposed `skariondrafting.com`, pending owner and registrar review. Build output can run at that root once approved. If kept on the parent site, use `/cad-drafting-as-builts/`. Confirm canonical URL and share card after hosting is chosen. Inquiries open an email draft to `info@skarion.com`. Confirm mailbox routing, reviewer assignment, inquiry handling and secure drawing transfer before launch.
