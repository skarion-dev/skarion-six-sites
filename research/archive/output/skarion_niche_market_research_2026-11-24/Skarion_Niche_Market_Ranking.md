The best commercial opportunities in this review were specialized infrastructure production offers where a buyer has a recurring obligation and a qualified prime can subcontract a defined batch. Skarion should test the five below in parallel through one GTM workflow. Public records give service-specific demand and several local competition benchmarks. Exact availability among approved subcontractors remains the question to answer with buyers.

Each prime response includes broader scope and qualifications than the proposed Skarion batch.

## Test for a real supplier gap

Set the same initial target of 20 sourced accounts in each of the five campaigns. Use utility GIS and service line records as distinct offers in the water channel. Use design, permitting, and closeout as distinct offers in the fiber channel. Give each account one owner, one first offer based on its current project phase, and a dated source. Count each buyer and procurement once across campaigns. Record warm and cold channels separately.

Ask the person who manages production how much work is waiting, how often it repeats, how many approved vendors can take it, and the lead time and quote range from those vendors. Verify the buyer can subcontract the batch and share the inputs. Ask what quality or schedule defect causes rework. A confirmed future work package, long qualified-vendor lead times, and a paid trial together make a stronger case than an isolated thin prime bid.

Promote campaigns based on qualified replies per delivered introduction, buyers reporting an unmet recurring queue, paid pilot requests, and contribution per senior review hour. Measure producer, reviewer, project management, and correction hours on actual delivery. Run multiple pilots when those reviewers have capacity. Pole records becomes the first alternate where an authorized utility prime can show a funded inventory or application backlog.

## How to read the scores

Demand and supply tightness use separate 1–5 evidence scales. Higher demand means stronger observed reachable buying. Higher tightness means signs of thinner qualified competition within the specified channel. An unknown private subcontract field stays unknown. Published contracts, seller directories, bid tabs, planholder logs, and hiring data measure different things. The shortlist reflects judgment about repeated buying, competition evidence, and Skarion’s workshare, without converting those signals into a national ratio. [Research method](00_Research_Method.md)
