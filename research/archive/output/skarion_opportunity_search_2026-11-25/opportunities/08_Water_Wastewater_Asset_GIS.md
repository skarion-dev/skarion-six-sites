## Pursuit sequence

1. Prepare a **25-record source-to-asset QA sample** using fabricated data. Include asset ID, source reference, attributes, QA outcome and unresolved exceptions. Use prime-provided data after authorization. Define acceptance as 100% source traceability, disposition for every exception and prime sign-off. Let Detroit’s lead name the actual batch and reviewer.
2. Check for any Marina Coast amendment and identify a committed team before November 30. Limit the scope to advisory QA and an editable checklist. In parallel, verify Daytona Beach Addendum 1 in the buyer portal and confirm whether McKim & Creed, Carollo or Wright-Pierce is actually bidding. A written task or proposal line item is the qualification threshold for either lead.
3. Use the same sample with Timmons for JEA validation or Indio as-needed support. For RFP-WM-3956, verify the owner’s record and identify field teams before it enters the active pursuit list. No prime-issued request for a GIS subcontract quote was verified in this pass.
