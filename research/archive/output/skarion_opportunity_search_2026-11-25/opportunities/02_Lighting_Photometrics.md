# 02 | Lighting layouts and photometrics

**US opportunity review · November 25, 2026**

Prioritize West Sacramento for a near-term pursuit if an eligible A&E prime attended the November 12 meeting. The solicitation calls for photometrics and a fixture schedule for two recreation-center buildings. Newport is the clearest named-prime route. Its public approval and proposal show how the prime budgeted a substantial streetlighting scope for an outside specialist. Champlin offers a five-year teaming route with a current December 11 response window.

For West Sacramento, the issuing buyer and project number are established. Multiple copies of the procurement record show a December 11 deadline and the work description, but OpenGov blocked retrieval of the live project page in this review. Funding amount, actual prime attendance, current addenda and a Skarion work order remain unverified. The owner requests photometrics and schedules as project tasks. Separating them into a desktop subcontract is a proposed workshare.

## Pursuit sequence

1. **West Sacramento first.** Check the meeting roster, recent addenda, bidder questions and permitted contact route. When a qualified attendee is identified, offer a bounded fixture schedule and calculation package for KA/KB that can fit its submission or delivery plan. A no-attendance or already-complete team moves this lead to award watch.
2. **VN Engineers and Summit.** Reuse one sample for each specific application. For VN, show a roadway/sidewalk grid with model assumptions, average/minimum and uniformity outputs, fixture selection inputs and a CAD plan overlay. For Summit, show a parking-lot retrofit using a short CAD legend, fixture replacement pairs and a property-line calculation grid. Quote a paid batch once the responsible engineer supplies criteria, site geometry and fixture data.
3. **Champlin and upcoming awards.** Pursue a state-aid-experienced prime before Dec 11. Recheck Ridgefield by project number for a decision already scheduled, and Burlingame around the December interviews and January award window. Keep engineering decisions, field verification and required seals with the qualified project team. Confirm owner and prime authorization for any subcontract and file transfer before work.

The public sample contains two future-deadline buyer records, two approved project leads with named specialist workshare, and two closed award watches. No current prime-issued request for a standalone photometric or CAD quote was verified. These records establish that primes buy specialist lighting work. They do not measure supplier scarcity or identify available subcontract capacity.
