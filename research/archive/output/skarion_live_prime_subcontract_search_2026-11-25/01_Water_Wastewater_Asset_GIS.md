# Water and wastewater asset GIS

**U.S. prime quote search · November 25, 2026**

I found no publicly verified, open prime solicitation for a standalone water/wastewater GIS or records-QA package. **PM Construction & Rehab / PURIS** is the strongest immediate call. Its active sewer inspection requests have a named quote contact, and the same contact has previously advertised work that pairs inspections with GIS data collection. **XL Eagle** publishes an active price intake for Daytona's owner RFQ, which explicitly includes GIS. XL Eagle’s professional-engineering eligibility and actual bid participation are unverified.
