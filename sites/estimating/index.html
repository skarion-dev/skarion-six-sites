Process

From released plans to your bid desk.

  1. Confirm scope

    Agree on plan revision, latest addendum, bid schedule, time zone and work limits.

  2. Measure and check

    Map each measurement to a bid item. A second reviewer spot-checks quantities and references.

  3. Hand off and clarify

    Receive the workbook, marked plans and open issues with one agreed clarification cycle.
