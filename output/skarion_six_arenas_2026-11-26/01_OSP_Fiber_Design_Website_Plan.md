**Pursue now. Internal plan for manual review.** Sell controlled route-design production to US broadband operators, design primes and fiber construction teams. Start with one route or design area whose inputs, receiving system and design reviewer are already known.

## Buyers and work packages

| Offer | First buyer | Inputs and boundaries | Proposed output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid route pilot** | OSP design manager or prime PM facing a defined backlog | One approved route segment, field references, drafting standards, required file versions, review owner and acceptance criteria | Small native plan package plus PDF, BOM extract if scoped, exception register and one consolidated review cycle |
| **HLD and LLD production** | ISP or prime that needs recurring area-based production | Approved service area, approved network architecture and design criteria, current field inputs and agreed work limits. Route alternatives and engineering judgments follow the buyer's review process | HLD or LLD, aerial and underground plan sheets, crossing or bore details from approved criteria, splice and material schedules, and the approved GIS exchange format |
| **Revision and overflow capacity** | Engineering PM or construction manager managing field comments and issue-for-construction dates | Existing drawings, consolidated redlines, revision controls and expected queue. Agree priority and reviewer capacity before committing a turnaround | Revised native files and PDFs, sheet index, comment log and unresolved issues register |

Route a labor- and unit-cost request to **OSP and utility bid estimating**. Route final installed-condition datasets, asset schemas and accepted splice/test file reconciliation to **SkarionGIS**. The OSP reviewer owns route logic, connection continuity, sheet coordination and scoped mapping outputs. The client or retained licensed professional controls field certification, design decisions, required sign-off and permit submission under the project agreement.

## Team review and first production trial

| Owner | Manual review action | What clears it |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial lead | Confirm buyers, pilot minimum scope, pricing assumptions and scope boundaries. Review both first-party direct peers and actual potential primes in the comparison sheet. | One-page scope and quote assumptions validated against internal cost and actual reviewer capacity. |
| OSP delivery lead | Produce the synthetic artifacts, identify supported versions and test the receiving-file handoff. | No mismatched IDs or unresolved critical geometry, continuity or export defects. Any remaining technical decisions are marked for the client's authorized reviewer. |
| Responsible reviewer | Approve technical claims, QA wording, data-location and design approval copy. Document the authority that will sign off on customer work. | Signed internal review record and approved set of claims. |
| Website and marketing owner | Build staging content, check mobile and desktop, tag the form and test lead routing. Verify usage rights for every image and case. | Workflow tested end to end, sample pages approved, and legal ownership labels visible to visitors. |

For the first paid trial, use one scoped segment with a fixed drawing and revision limit, one authorized reviewer, time tracked for production and review, a defined response window and an agreed approval record. Judge it on accepted deliverables, defect/rework rate and repeat work or a wider paid request. The clearest project-backed routing leads are HR Green's Duluth team and the named Broadlinc awardees.
