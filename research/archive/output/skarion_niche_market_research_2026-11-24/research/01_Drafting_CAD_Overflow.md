# Drafting and CAD overflow

**Demand 4/5, high confidence. Supply tightness 2/5, medium-high confidence. Accessibility high for privately commissioned drawing production and conditional for agency-restricted or controlled records.** Buyers regularly outsource drafting, and the evidence includes repeat purchase orders. Generic conversion faces deep competition. The better Skarion offer is a repeatable batch of redlines and record drawings delivered to the buyer’s standards, where private vendor capacity remains unmeasured.

## Offer and buyers

Take one batch of existing DWGs or dimensioned record drawings, plus marked-up sheets, from a US engineering consultant, general contractor, architecture practice, or facilities records team. Return editable DWGs, plotted PDFs, a revision log, and a list of unresolved dimensions or conflicting instructions. The customer supplies the source records, scale controls, standards, and design decisions. Weekly construction revisions, renovation delivery peaks, and asset closeout create repeat triggers. Use distinct scopes for existing DWG changes, clean vector PDFs, and raster redraws. AutoCAD can extract existing vector geometry from a PDF, while raster scans require a separate conversion or redraw process and independent dimension checks. [Autodesk PDF import documentation](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/AutoCAD-Core/files/GUID-1202CC8A-364F-4E93-8E86-6F476CD83C72.htm)

Reachable demand means paid production accounts that accept the proposed delivery model and can provide suitable source files. Filter out scopes that require substantive design judgments, unallocated field measurements, or specialist CAD software absent from the delivery team. Count trade closeout drafting separately from telecom closeout, water GIS, or a full BIM model when the work packages have different deliverables and buyers.

## Campaign decision

Use drafting as the **high-demand, high-competition benchmark**. Keep managed redline and record drawing batches available as a campaign because clients directly purchase the scope and Skarion can use existing skills. Give a higher top-five priority to a niche with equally reachable demand and stronger evidence of qualified scarcity. Public utility and federal files establish an attractive specialization for providers with suitable systems and references. For Skarion, buyer-permitted private production avoids some acquisition barriers and still requires a production lead, reviewer, approved sample, and clear data terms.

The next decisive information is whether private AEC buyers can name **an upcoming backlog they would outsource, their alternative supplier’s availability, and the terms of the second order**. Use the 20-account research batch to find buyers with project triggers and comparable alternatives. Quote a trial of one simple existing-conditions sheet at the prior **$149 hypothesis** once input quality is validated. For infrastructure primes, separately quote a three-to-five-sheet redline or record package with an exception log and stated acceptance criteria. Record actual production, review, and rework time, realized contribution, and whether the buyer places another batch.
