## First steps

1. **For exact desktop photometrics**, qualify the existing need with VN and KL. Use one sample showing the approved IES file, CAD geometry, grid averages/minimums/uniformity, boundary calculations where relevant, and a fixture/revision log. Confirm the package and reviewer before preparing a priced scope.
2. **For dated quotes**, confirm XL Eagle’s actual team and cutoff on West Sacramento and Kentucky. Treat SFTC and Kiewit as electrical construction-first approaches. A qualified prime or its electrical subcontractor has to accept a separable drawing package.
3. **For award tracking**, use the owner’s addenda or notice of intent before treating bidders as selected. Owner and prime permission, qualified engineering review, delivery location, licensure and data sharing should be checked before requesting project files.
