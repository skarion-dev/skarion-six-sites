## Action rules for Zihad / OpenClaw

1. **Recheck every deadline/status from the buyer portal on each review**; don't assume a future deadline means all mandatory requirements are still achievable.
2. **Resolve actual bidder eligibility first.** Confirm insurance, licenses, certifications, portfolio, contract registrations, references, field presence, and offshore/subcontracting restrictions.
3. **Prioritize profitable design/data work**: OSP HLD/LLD and fiber allocations, CAD/Civil 3D only with qualified staff, GIS cleanup, data conversion, cartography, as-builts, and construction drawing QC.
4. **Separate prime bids from teaming targets.** Do not imply Skarion provides construction crews, survey-grade field collection, traffic signal engineering, remote sensing expertise, or licensed professional signoff unless those capabilities are genuinely available.
5. **Track an evidence link and date last checked** beside each opportunity. Use status: `Verify now`, `Go`, `Conditional`, `Teaming only`, `No-go`, `Submitted`, `Won/Lost`.
6. **Escalate time-sensitive submissions and approvals.** No paid registration, outreach, or binding bid submission without management authorization.

**Note on budgets:** No unverified aggregator-generated contract-value estimates are treated as actual buyer budgets in this inventory.
