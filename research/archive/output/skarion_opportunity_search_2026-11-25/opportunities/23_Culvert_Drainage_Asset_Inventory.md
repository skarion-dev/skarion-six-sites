## Pursuit sequence

1. Qualify Black & Veatch and Stillwater first. Ask for the expected handoff date, number of unprocessed assets, exact schema, file custody, permitted staff location, reviewer, and approved spend. Check Rio Vista in parallel before the anticipated December start.
2. Confirm whether the OHLA solicitation is active, then check separate-scope and eligibility conditions before the advertised Dec 2 quote deadline. For Ayden and Manchester, determine whether a qualified prime intends to bid and needs a named inventory production sub.
3. Offer a paid, 25–50-asset trial with a versioned editable layer, source and photo links, schema/check results, and an exceptions register using prime-approved inputs and condition codes. Set price from record quality, volume and accepted output. Leave field inspections, professional assessments and sign-off with the qualified party.
