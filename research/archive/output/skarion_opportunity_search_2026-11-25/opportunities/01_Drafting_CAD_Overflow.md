## Pursuit sequence

1. **Qualify KGM before December 2.** Obtain the as-built deliverable and certification rules from the estimator and procurement files. Prepare a drafting estimate only if approved field sources, a desktop workshare and an eligible route are confirmed.
2. **Prioritize GHD closeout.** Ask for the redline release schedule, sheet count, CAD version, XREF and layer standards, lead reviewer, data access and approved work location. Sample offer is one fixed-fee checked batch, native drawings and plot PDFs plus an exceptions log. GHD would retain responsibility for engineering acceptance.
3. **Follow the WSDOT shortlist and Cary selection.** Capture official team or award documents, and pitch repetitive redline production only to relevant confirmed primes. The WSDOT vehicle gives the best repeat-order structure in this sample. Confirm task-order scope and volume before projecting revenue.
4. **Track the VA awards as gated leads.** Record awardee and workshare eligibility before spending bid time. These records show buyer demand for drawing updates, while the sample leaves qualified supplier counts, award competition and actual drafting subcontract prices unknown.
