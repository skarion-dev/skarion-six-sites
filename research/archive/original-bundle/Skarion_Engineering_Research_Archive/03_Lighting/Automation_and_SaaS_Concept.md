# Lighting workflow automation / SaaS concept
Prior VCL context documented a human-reviewed workflow: intake, base drawing and revision tracking, CAD cleanup, ordinance research, photometric/electrical checks, cross-check, permit package, plan-check response, cost estimating. The original VCL handoff is restricted internal reference; do not copy third-party rulebooks, project drawings, private employee details, code or IP into Skarion products without authorization.

## Possible Skarion-owned MVP (concept, not validated product)
1. Intake client-approved plans, IES files, project metadata and revision history.
2. Extract fixture schedule and flag missing/conflicting information; maintain a transparent source trail.
3. Generate QA checklist and draft report from approved rules; require qualified reviewer approval.
4. Track revisions and create transmittal package. No autonomous certification, sealed plans or claims of code compliance.

Potential customers: lighting distributors with repeat layouts, specialist consultants, small MEP firms and retrofit contractors. Validate their current workflow, hours lost, data security and willingness to pay before developing integrations. Use internal production first; monetize only after repeat demonstrated value. UK/EU expansion needs local standards, privacy and market validation. Exact prior international segmentation research was not recovered; this section is a synthesis, not a verbatim reconstruction.
