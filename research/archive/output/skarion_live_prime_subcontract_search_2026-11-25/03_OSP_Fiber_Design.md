## Immediate pitch

Lead with one serving-area pilot. Convert an approved high-level route and field data into design plan sheets, splice/strand and material schedules, a native CAD/GIS handoff and an issue register. Ask which budgeted package is open, who owns vendor approval, when they stop taking quotes, and where the data may be accessed. Keep licensed decisions and field validation with the authorized project team. Public-source checks covered retained owner RFPs, firm websites and published recruitment, DBE and contractor trade ads, state advertising, public planroom traces, and indexed SBA SubNet results. The SubNet application did not load and private invited-bid portals were unavailable, which limits coverage of unpublished work.
